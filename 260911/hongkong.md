# Hong Kong Stock Market Analysis — September 11, 2026 (Friday) — SESSION IN PROGRESS

> **This file is an intraday snapshot, not a confirmed close.** The Hong Kong market (closes 4:00pm local time / 4:00pm Taipei time) was still trading at the time this was researched. A follow-up pass is needed after the close (see `README.md`'s "Follow-up needed" section) to replace this with confirmed closing data.

## What's known so far (intraday)

Hong Kong equities extended their recent losing streak Friday, tracking the same oil-price surge and rising US Treasury yields hitting nearly every market this week:

- The **Hang Seng Index opened down 0.95% (to 24,718.15)** and traded in a roughly **−0.85% to −1% range** through the midday break and early afternoon (readings ranged 24,691–24,772 across several intraday snapshots), while the **Hang Seng Tech Index fell between roughly −0.79% and −1.36%** across the same window.
- **Brent crude surged above $109/barrel intraday** — a fresh high for the ongoing Strait of Hormuz escalation — and the **US 10-year Treasury yield moved closer to 5%**, both cited directly as pressuring Hong Kong and mainland Chinese equities (Shanghai Composite −1.82%, CSI 300 −1.59% intraday).
- **Commodity/metals-linked names were the sharpest decliners**: **Jiangxi Copper and Luoyang Molybdenum both fell more than 10% intraday**, and **Zijin Mining fell more than 6%**, even as **COMEX gold fell 2.29%** — a genuine paradox where gold-and-metals miners sold off even as the broader macro backdrop (oil shock, geopolitical risk) would typically be read as gold-supportive, consistent with this week's recurring pattern of higher-for-longer rate expectations outweighing safe-haven demand (see `usa.md`).
- **Property developers fell 3–6%**, and **technology names Kuaishou and Lenovo both fell more than 2%**, rounding out a broadly negative, multi-sector session.
- Same-day Stock Connect (港股通 / 南向資金) southbound net-flow figures were not available from sources checked during market hours, consistent with this file's usual same-day limitation.
