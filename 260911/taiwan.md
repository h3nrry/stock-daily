# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 11, 2026 (Friday)

## Headline

Taiwan's TAIEX fell sharply as a fresh leg higher in oil prices and rising US Treasury yields drove a broad, cautious retreat ahead of the US Consumer Price Index report due after Taiwan's close. The index **closed at 46,184.85, down 755.64 points (−1.61%)**, after tumbling as much as **900-plus points intraday to a low of 45,964** — briefly breaching both the psychological 46,000 level and its own monthly moving average (~46,017–46,025) — before recovering to finish just above that monthly support line. Turnover came in at **NT$721.908 billion**. The close reconciles exactly against Thursday's confirmed 46,940.49 (see `../260910/taiwan.md`).

## What drove the decline

- **Oil's latest surge intensified investor caution globally**: international crude jumped more than 6% intraday, breaking above $100/barrel, as the US-Iran Strait of Hormuz standoff continued to escalate (see `usa.md`) — the same story that has driven nearly every market covered in this project for over a week.
- **The US Federal Reserve's September 15–16 policy meeting and, more immediately, tonight's US CPI report (8:30pm Taipei time)** kept the market firmly in "wait-and-see" mode; strategist Yellow Kuo (郭恒宗) noted the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) "fell but did not break its bottom," reading Friday's pullback as a pause for clearer signals rather than a capitulation.
- **TSMC / 台積電 (2330) led the decline**, closing at **NT$2,410 (−NT$40, −1.63%)** and alone dragging the index down roughly **317 points**; **MediaTek / 聯發科 (2454), Delta Electronics / 台達電 (2308), and ASE Technology / 日月光投控 (3711)** combined for a further **~108-point drag**, with MediaTek closing at NT$4,585 (−135) and Delta at NT$1,620 (−55).
- **Financial stocks tried to cushion the fall**: Mega Financial (兆豐金) and E.SUN Financial (玉山金) among others contributed roughly **+30 points** collectively, not nearly enough to offset the tech-led slide.
- **High-volume gainers bucked the tide**: 光頡 (3624), 高力 (8996), 鼎元 (2426), 陽明 (2609), 聯一光 (3441), 光罩 (2338), 研華 (2395), 青雲 (5386), and 台勝科 (3532); **high-volume decliners** besides TSMC/MediaTek/Delta included 世芯-KY (3661), 華新科 (2492), 聯電 (2303), 友達 (2409), 華邦電 (2344), and 南亞 (1303).
- **The New Taiwan dollar weakened alongside stocks** — a genuine stocks-and-currency "double sell-off" (股匯雙殺) — falling more than 1.3角 (0.13 NT$) intraday to around **NT$31.6–31.684 per US dollar**, directly tracking the oil-driven risk-off tone.

A dramatically heavier net-sell day than Thursday's already-negative −NT$50.311 billion (see `../260910/taiwan.md`): foreign investors alone sold more than double Thursday's amount, and dealers' hedging book was the single largest contributor to their own −NT$30.202 billion outflow. Only investment trusts stayed net buyers, and even their buying (+NT$7.10 billion) was smaller than Thursday's. Official TWSE data (see Reference sources) confirms this is one of the heavier institutional-selling sessions of the past two weeks, consistent with the "wait-and-see ahead of CPI" narrative in local commentary.

## Bottom line

A genuinely risk-off session with real institutional confirmation behind it — TAIEX −1.61% to 46,184.85, its sharpest single-day decline in over a week, as oil's fresh surge above $100/barrel and a "wait-and-see" posture ahead of tonight's US CPI report drove the heaviest foreign-investor net-selling day (−NT$89.27 billion) of recent sessions. TSMC's ~317-point index drag was the single clearest story, though financial-stock resilience and a late recovery off the 45,964 intraday low — which briefly breached both 46,000 and the monthly moving average — kept the close from being worse. With the Fed's September 15–16 meeting and tonight's inflation data both looming, whether Friday's pullback resolves into stabilization or the start of a deeper rotation is the clearest thing to watch into next week.
