## What's expected at today's open

- **Pre-market futures point to a modestly firmer open**: **Dow futures +125 points (+0.24%) to 52,220** as of writing, a tentative reversal of Thursday's four-day losing streak — though this reading predates tonight's CPI release and could shift sharply either direction once that data lands.
- **Tonight's August CPI report (8:30am ET / 8:30pm Taipei) is the single biggest scheduled catalyst of the week.** Consensus expects **headline inflation +0.4% m/m and +3.4% y/y, core +0.4% m/m and +2.4% y/y** — both a re-acceleration from July's softer readings (headline +0.1% m/m / 3.4% y/y; core +0.2% m/m / 2.5% y/y). The report lands squarely ahead of next week's **September 15–16 FOMC meeting**, where hike odds already stood near **70% per CME FedWatch** as of Thursday.
- **Oil remained the dominant cross-market story**: Brent crude pushed above **$109/barrel intraday** in Asian trading hours — a fresh high in the escalating US-Iran Strait of Hormuz standoff — before easing to around **$105.50** on the latest reading (a −1.98% pullback from the intraday peak); WTI traded near **$103.11 (+0.61%)** on the day. The conflict has featured US strikes on Iran and reported strikes on Saudi Aramco facilities in recent sessions (see [[project_stock_sources]]'s Iran/Hormuz tracking category).
- **US Treasury yields stayed near multi-year highs**: the 10-year was reported approaching **5%**, among its highest levels in nearly three years, extending Thursday's already-elevated close (4.954%) — a disappointing long-bond auction overnight added further upward pressure.
- **Asia traded sharply lower ahead of the US open**: TAIEX −1.61%, KOSPI −1.76% (both partially recovering from deeper intraday losses), and Nikkei −1.93% (Advantest alone responsible for roughly 617 points of that decline) — see `taiwan.md`, `korea.md`, `japan.md` — all reading the same oil/yields/CPI-anticipation script that will likely shape tonight's US session too.

## Oil, gold, bitcoin (latest available reads)

- **Oil**: Brent touched above **$109/barrel** intraday during Asian hours before easing to roughly **$105.50 (−1.98% off the intraday peak)**; WTI traded near **$103.11 (+0.61% on the day)** — the ongoing Strait of Hormuz standoff remains the dominant driver, with both benchmarks still well above levels seen before this story began escalating in early September.
- **Gold**: XAU/USD held near **$4,330**, down from Thursday's close of $4,407.30 (roughly −1.7%), as strengthening Fed-hike expectations (on the back of Thursday's hot PPI) and elevated Treasury yields continue to outweigh the metal's usual safe-haven bid even amid the live geopolitical/oil story — tonight's CPI print is described as gold's "next major catalyst." **Silver** was reported down roughly −0.5% on the same read.
- **Bitcoin**: traded near **$77,063 (−1.73% on a 24-hour basis)** as of the most recent available read (~2:19am Taipei time) — continuing the softness seen through Thursday's session. Since bitcoin trades 24/7, this is a point-in-time read rather than a session close, and a fresher figure should be pulled when this file is completed post-close.
