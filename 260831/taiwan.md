# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — August 31, 2026 (Monday)

## Headline

**TAIEX (加權指數) fell 202.98 points (−0.44%) to close at 46,128.47**, on trading value of **NT$1.20 trillion (US$37.97 billion)**. It was a genuinely volatile session — the index sank over **880 points intraday**, briefly breaking below 46,000 to touch a low of **45,450.21**, before clawing back more than half the loss into the close on bargain-hunting and technical support at the 10-day moving average (~45,438). The session opened the week under pressure from Friday's US selloff (Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole tone, see `usa.md`) and a fresh spike in oil prices after **US military strikes on Iranian targets over the weekend** re-escalated Middle East tensions — but Taiwan-specific support (MSCI passive-fund buying tied to Monday's weighting increase) helped the index recover most of its early losses.

## What drove the index

- **MSCI rebalancing effective after Monday's close** was the single biggest technical driver of the day: passive index funds tracking MSCI added to Taiwan positions ahead of the country's weighting increase, and this buying — concentrated into the final trading session — helped the index recover roughly half of its intraday 880-point drop. **TSMC (台積電, 2330) saw an explosive burst of over 14,000 lots traded in the final minutes** as MSCI-linked flows rebalanced.
- **Semiconductors were mixed**: TSMC fell a modest **0.62% to NT$2,405** (down NT$15/NT$20 depending on source), MediaTek (聯發科) declined **1.51% (down NT$60 to NT$3,925)**, while smaller names like Alchip (世芯) recovered intraday to close **+0.25%**.
- **Petrochemicals were the day's standout sector, +3.51%** — Nan Ya Plastics (南亞) surged **+10% (limit-up)** and Formosa Plastics (台塑) jumped **+5.58%**, likely benefiting from the oil-price spike improving petrochemical product-price spreads.
- **Shipping rallied on rising freight rates**: Evergreen Marine (長榮海運) +0.86%, Yang Ming (陽明海運) +5.87%.
- **Financials gained 1.88%**, adding broad-based support beneath the tech-led weakness.
- **Delta Electronics (台達電, 2308)** bucked the tech weakness, up NT$10 to NT$1,840.
- **Notable decliner: Unimicron Technology (欣興電子)** tumbled **10%** after prosecutors opened an investigation into alleged product mislabeling.

## Bottom line

A genuinely two-sided session: TAIEX opened the week absorbing Friday's hawkish-Fed shock and a fresh Iran-driven oil spike, sliding as much as 880 points intraday, but MSCI passive-fund buying tied to Monday's weighting increase — landing hardest in the final minutes on TSMC — helped claw back more than half the loss by the close. Petrochemicals and shipping, direct beneficiaries of the oil spike, were the day's clear sector winners, while semiconductors broadly lagged. With foreign investors net sellers by a modest NT$14.39B (a sharp comedown from Friday's record NT$366B buying, but far from a rout), Monday reads as consolidation after two straight weeks of gains rather than a trend break — the open question is whether the weekend's Iran escalation (see `usa.md` and the README) becomes a sustained macro headwind into September.
