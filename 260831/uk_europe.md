# UK & Europe Stock Market Analysis — August 31, 2026 (Monday)

## Headline

European markets closed **mixed to lower**, digesting the same weekend Iran-strike/oil-price shock and post-Jackson-Hole hawkish Fed repricing weighing on global markets. **Germany's DAX fell 1.17% to 26,258.11**, **France's CAC 40 declined 0.79% to 8,334.50**, and **Britain's FTSE 100 held up better, closing at approximately 10,824.26** (percent change not separately confirmed, but the level is essentially flat vs. recent sessions).

## What drove the market

- **Oil-price spike**: Brent crude's jump to $93.03/barrel following the weekend's US strikes on Iran (see `../usa.md`) weighed on rate-sensitive and growth-oriented sectors across Europe, mirroring the pattern seen in Asia and the US.
- **DAX underperformance**: Germany's index, more industrial- and export-weighted, bore the brunt of the day's selling — a pattern consistent with global growth-sensitivity concerns tied to higher energy costs.
- **FTSE relative resilience**: London's heavier weighting toward energy majors and defensives likely explains its comparative outperformance versus Frankfurt and Paris on a day when oil prices spiked.

## Bottom line

A straightforwardly risk-off session for continental Europe (DAX and CAC both down around 0.8–1.2%), with London's energy/defensive tilt providing relative shelter from the same Iran-driven oil shock rattling markets worldwide.
