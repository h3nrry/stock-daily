# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 7, 2026 (Monday)

## Headline

Taiwan opened the trading week with a powerful rally that fully validated the bullish overnight-futures signal flagged ahead of the session (Friday night's 台指期夜盤 was up +1.0% over the prior cash close — see the prior pre-market outlook, preserved in project history and summarized in `README.md`). The **TAIEX (加權指數) surged 775.14 points (+1.66%) to close at 47,326.27**, its highest close in over a month and a clean break above the psychological 47,000 level, on turnover of roughly **NT$939.9–941.4 billion** (sources checked differ slightly on the exact figure). The intraday range spanned **46,724 to 47,429.58**.

## What drove the rally

- **Semiconductors led decisively, exactly as Friday's SOX/TSMC-ADR surge and the overnight futures premium had signaled**: **MediaTek / 聯發科 (2454) surged +7.81%**, approaching the daily limit-up, on optimism about its shift toward data-center custom AI chips entering mass production in Q4; **TSMC / 台積電 (2330) rose +2.07% to NT$2,460**, benefiting from continued AI/high-speed-computing-driven advanced-process demand; **ASE Technology / 日月光投控 (3711) gained +5.61%**, with advanced packaging remaining a focus as AI chips drive larger package sizes. Multiple other semiconductor and materials names — including **UMC / 聯電** — also surged more than 5%.
- **A sharp divergence in optics/interconnect**: **Largan Precision / 大立光 (3008) collapsed through the NT$7,000 level to hit limit-down (跌停) at NT$6,660**, after a European brokerage downgrade argued the co-packaged-optics (CPO) growth narrative was "already reflected" in the stock's price — a reminder that Monday's rally, while broad across the semiconductor complex, left optical/interconnect names as the clear laggard.
- **Breadth was narrower than the headline number suggests**: across the combined main and OTC boards, **808 stocks advanced versus 1,068 that declined** (30 limit-up, 4 limit-down) — meaning more individual names fell than rose even as the index itself jumped 1.66%, a pattern consistent with a rally concentrated in a handful of large-cap semiconductor names.

## vs. Monday's pre-market outlook

The pre-market outlook flagged Taiwan as "the clearest bullish setup on the board," pointing to Friday's +1.0% overnight-futures premium and one analyst's illustrative 48,000-point target. **The actual session confirmed the bullish direction emphatically** — TAIEX +1.66% to 47,326.27, with a record institutional net-buy day — though it fell short of the analyst's 48,000 target (and of a short-squeeze scenario). The market-based futures signal proved the more reliable of the two pre-market reads, exactly as the outlook itself had cautioned it might be.

## Bottom line

A genuinely confirmed, institutionally-backed rally — TAIEX +1.66% to a fresh one-month high on the largest net institutional buy day in this project's history (NT$113.865 billion), led by MediaTek and TSMC on AI/data-center optimism. The one clear crack beneath the headline number: breadth was negative (more decliners than advancers) and Largan Precision's limit-down shows the CPO/optical trade may be due for a rethink even as the broader semiconductor complex extends its rally. Whether Monday's institutional conviction carries into the rest of the week — set against Friday's US CPI print as the week's real resolution point — is the clearest thing to watch next.
