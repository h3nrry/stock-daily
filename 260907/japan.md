# Japan Stock Market Analysis — September 7, 2026 (Monday)

## Headline

Japan's Nikkei 225 extended Friday's rebound with an even bigger gain to open the trading week. The index **closed at 66,399.84, up 1,378.90 points (+2.12%)** — its second consecutive strong session and, combined with Friday's +1.26% gain, a two-day advance of roughly ¥2,185 points that has now erased more than the entire four-session losing streak from earlier in the week (see `../260904/japan.md`).

## What drove the rally

- **Broad, sustained AI/semiconductor buying** carried directly from Friday's session, itself a read-through from Thursday's US rally and Friday's SOX/TSMC-ADR surge (see `../260904/usa.md`) — with US markets closed for Labor Day, Monday's Tokyo session had no fresh Wall Street cue and instead ran on its own momentum plus the region's own upbeat mood (Korea's KOSPI +4.61% and Taiwan's TAIEX +1.66% the same day).
- **Kioxia led in trading volume** and **SoftBank Group posted the day's top gains**, continuing to be the single most important stock for the index's direction, exactly as it was on Friday.
- **Chip-equipment makers rallied broadly**: **Advantest, Tokyo Electron, and Laser Tec** all advanced, tracking the same AI-capex optimism that has driven much of the Nikkei's volatility (in both directions) this year.
- **Breadth stayed narrow despite the strong headline number, echoing Friday's pattern**: on the Tokyo Prime Market, **633 issues advanced versus 885 that declined** — meaning 57% of all trading names ended below Friday's close even as the index itself jumped over 2%. The top five index contributors alone added an estimated **~1,328 points**, underscoring how concentrated the gain was in a small number of mega-cap names.

## Money flow — 外国人 (foreign investors)

Same-day foreign-investor flow data via JPX's weekly investor-type statistics remained unavailable at writing time (JPX publishes weekly, not daily — the same limitation flagged in prior files). Given the pattern of a narrow, mega-cap-and-semiconductor-concentrated rally with more decliners than advancers, Monday's move reads as a continuation of concentrated buying in a handful of AI/chip-related names rather than a broad-based foreign-investor return — consistent with Friday's read, but this remains an inference from price action and breadth, not a confirmed flow figure.

## Bottom line

A second consecutive strong session, extending Friday's rebound to a two-day gain of roughly 2,185 points — but the underlying breadth tells a more cautious story than the headline number: for two sessions running, more Tokyo Prime Market stocks have fallen than risen even as the index itself surged. With SoftBank Group again the single largest driver and US markets closed for Labor Day removing the usual overnight cross-check, whether this rally broadens beyond a handful of AI/semiconductor mega-caps is the clearest thing to watch into Tuesday.
