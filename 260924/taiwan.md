# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 24, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

The **TAIEX fell 132.69 points (−0.28%) to 48,024.60**, ending a six-day winning streak but **holding above 48,000** (險守4萬8大關) on the **last session before a four-day holiday** (TWSE closed Fri Sept 25 for Mid-Autumn Festival and Mon Sept 28 for Teachers' Day; it reopens Tue Sept 29). The index opened lower, fell as much as **~378 points** in the morning (low **47,757.42**) with **TSMC leading the decline**, then recovered most of the loss. **Turnover fell to NT$736.62 billion** (量縮) as traders waited before the holiday. **For the week (four sessions), the TAIEX gained 843.85 points (+1.79%)**, its second straight weekly gain. **Institutions turned net sellers (−NT$44.449 billion)**, ending the five-day foreign buying streak.

## What drove the session

- **TSMC (台積電, 2330) fell NT$25 (−1.0%) to NT$2,475** after the US rates shock overnight (10Y at a 2007 high, Nasdaq −1.13%, SOX −1.23%; see `../260923/usa.md`). TVBS said memory stocks were "all green" (記憶體一片綠) early in the session. In Hong Kong the same day, memory-chip stocks fell after reports that **Michael Burry had added to his short position in Micron** and warned of a cycle reversal (see `hongkong.md`).
- **MediaTek (聯發科, 2454) went against the trend, +NT$100 (+1.93%) to NT$5,285**, its highest close yet (UDN: "發哥續衝", "聯發科翻紅抗空").
- **AI-server cooling and substrates led the gainers:** **Auras (雙鴻, 3324) +~8%** and **Jentech (健策, 3653) +~6%**; **Kinsus (景碩, 3189) +~6%**, approaching NT$1,000; **ITEQ (聯茂, 6213) limit-up** (CCL materials); **Unimicron (欣興, 3037)** and **AVC (奇鋐, 3017)** were among the largest positive contributors to the index (Cnyes).
- **Electronics assemblers fell:** **Hon Hai (鴻海, 2317) −2%+**, **Quanta (廣達, 2382) −~1%**. Yahoo/Anue's "三大法人24日賣超前十大" headline described electronics and financials as falling together (電金難兄難弟).
- **Holiday caution.** Local media (Cnyes, UDN, CTI) framed the session as position-cutting before the break (長假前觀望), with the Trump–Xi summit happening during Taiwan's closure. CTI's morning note warned about "a shake-out before the holiday" (慎防秋節變盤甩轎).

Still, the timing matters: it came right after the US 10-year hit 5.1%.

## Bottom line

Taiwan closed the four-day week **at 48,024.60 (−0.28% on the day, +1.79% on the week)**, holding 48,000 after a morning drop of ~378 points. **Institutions sold NT$44.4 billion before the holiday** (foreign −NT$33.0bn, trust −NT$12.8bn), and trading volume fell. Record highs in MediaTek and AI-cooling/substrate stocks show that money is rotating within tech rather than leaving it. Taiwan now misses the Trump–Xi summit and two US sessions, and reopens Sept 29, the day before Micron's earnings.
