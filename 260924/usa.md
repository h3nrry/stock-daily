## What's driving the session

- **Rates are still the main story.** **10Y 5.11–5.15% (highest since 2007), 2Y ≈4.90% (highest since 2023), 30Y ≈5.44% (highest since 2004)** (TheStreet, Trading Economics). October hike odds are about **64–75%** depending on the source (TE ~64%, CME FedWatch via Yahoo 75.3%, TheStreet ~70%). **Weekly jobless claims fell to 197,000** from 202,000 (Schwab), another sign of a tight labor market and more support for further hikes.
- **Worries about funding for AI infrastructure:** **Oracle −4% to −6.4%** after a **force-majeure notice on its New Mexico data center**; Blue Owl −4.7% and Bloom Energy −5% early (Newsquawk). **Arm and Intel −3% to −4%.**
- **Cybersecurity kept rising:** **Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike ~+5%** (Schwab), continuing Wednesday's AI-safety theme.
- **Single-stock moves:** Everpure +18.7% (reaffirmed FY27 guidance); Charles River Labs +5.4% (raised outlook); **MGM Resorts −10%** (Barry Diller's $18B buyout withdrawn); **TD Synnex −13%** (sold off after earnings); **McDonald's ~−5%** (2030 margin targets); Starbucks −0.6% (closing ~250 stores). **Meta** unveiled cameraless VR glasses and plans to make money from its Muse AI agent.
- **Trump–Xi summit:** Treasury Secretary Bessent said the **US–China "Busan Agreement" truce is extended two months to Jan 10, 2027**. The Oval Office meeting is at 11:30am ET, then an expanded meeting at noon, and a state dinner with tech and banking CEOs on Sept 25. Trump said he does **not** expect progress on AI guardrails. Other topics: trade (agriculture, low-tech consumer goods, possible tariff relief; US tariffs on Chinese goods average ~23% per Schwab), Taiwan, and China's support for Iran.
- **Oil kept rising:** Brent ≈$104.70 (+1.5%), WTI ≈$93–94.7 after aggressive Iranian statements about controlling the Strait of Hormuz. **Gold ≈$4,244–4,293 (−0.5% to −1.5%)** and **bitcoin ≈$83.5–83.8k (−2%)** fell with rising yields.
