# Korea Stock Market Analysis — September 24, 2026 (Thursday) — MARKET CLOSED (CHUSEOK)

## Bottom line

Korea is closed for Chuseok (Sept 24–25). The KOSPI's last close was **7,080.92 (+0.90%)** on Sept 23. Monday's reopening will have to catch up on a US yield spike to 2007 highs, oil back above $100, a two-month US–China truce extension, and Micron's upcoming earnings, all at once. That makes a large catch-up move likely in either direction, especially in Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix.
