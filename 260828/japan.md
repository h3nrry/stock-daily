# Japan Stock Market Analysis — August 28, 2026 (Friday)

## Headline

**Nikkei 225 closed at 66,405.56, up 273.58 points (+0.41%)**, rebounding from Thursday's small decline as IT, software, and select semiconductor names tracked overnight strength in US tech. The index climbed as much as 700+ points intraday before losing momentum into the close — investors grew cautious as the session wore on, with **Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole speech** (see `usa.md`) looming as the day's real wildcard. **TOPIX rose 0.72% to 4,146.71**, extending its winning streak to a seventh straight session — a broader and more consistent advance than the Nikkei's own volatile round-trip.

## What drove the gain

- **Information technology, software, and automaker shares led**, tracking the same enterprise-software strength (Salesforce, CrowdStrike) that powered Thursday's US rally (see `../260827/usa.md`) — a genuine cross-market echo of the software story, not just chips.
- **Advantest remained strong** and was again a key positive contributor, a reversal from Thursday's single-stock drag.
- **Tokyo Electron opened sharply higher but faded by the afternoon**, losing its early gains — part of the broader pattern of the index giving back roughly half its intraday advance.
- **Taiyo Yuden rose nearly 4%.**
- **Kioxia Holdings fell nearly 9%, despite Nvidia's earnings beat** — a notable divergence suggesting memory-specific concerns (pricing, supply) outweighed the broader AI-optimism read-through for this particular name.
- **SoftBank Group stayed heavy**, capping the index's upside.
- **Automakers gained broadly**: Toyota +1.8%, Honda +2.5%.
- **Caution ahead of Jackson Hole**: "investors remained cautious before Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole speech," with trading staying subdued into the close as market participants avoided aggressive positioning pending the US policy signal.

## Bottom line

A genuinely broad rebound — TOPIX's seven-session winning streak is the more telling signal than the Nikkei's own more volatile day, which surrendered roughly half of a 700-point intraday gain as caution set in ahead of Warsh's speech. The software/IT-led gains show Japan's market picking up the same secondary AI-adjacent story (enterprise software) that drove Thursday's US rally, broadening the region's AI trade beyond pure semiconductor plays — though Kioxia's near-9% decline despite Nvidia's own beat is a reminder that not every AI-supply-chain name is moving in lockstep. With Warsh's hawkish Friday remarks now known (see `usa.md`), next week's Japan session is the first real test of whether that caution was justified.
