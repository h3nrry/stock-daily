# Hong Kong Stock Market Analysis — August 28, 2026 (Friday)

## Headline

**Hang Seng Index closed at 25,593, up about 27 points (+0.11% to +0.30%)**, erasing an opening dip to end the week on a positive note. The gain came as "strong global technology sentiment following Nvidia's upbeat earnings and outlook provided support" — a delayed but real read-through of the week's dominant AI-earnings story finally showing up clearly in Hong Kong.

## What drove the gain — offshore money favored Hong Kong over Shanghai

The session's defining feature was a **divergence from mainland China**: Hong Kong "erased an opening dip to finish higher, while Shanghai closed lower" — coverage framed this explicitly as **"offshore money... picking Hong Kong"** rather than following the broader regional trend, suggesting international investors were deliberately rotating toward Hong Kong-listed shares over Shanghai-listed ones this session. This is a reversal of Thursday's pattern (`../260827/hongkong.md`), where Hong Kong had underperformed Shanghai.

## Bottom line

A quiet but genuinely positive close to the week, with Hong Kong finally showing the Nvidia-driven tech optimism that lifted much of the rest of the region earlier in the week, and a notable reversal of Thursday's underperformance versus mainland Shanghai listings as offshore capital rotated back toward Hong Kong. With Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole tone now known (see `usa.md`), next week's session will show whether Hong Kong's offshore-money tailwind can persist against a less accommodative macro backdrop.
