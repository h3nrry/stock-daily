# USA Stock Market Analysis — August 28, 2026 (Friday)

## Headline

US indices closed a strong week on a quieter, mixed note as markets digested Fed Chair **Kevin Warsh's first major Jackson Hole speech**. **Dow Jones closed at 53,598.14 (+0.05%)**, **S&P 500 at 7,722.06 (−0.12%)**, and the **Nasdaq Composite at 26,450.59 (−0.34%)** — early gains (the Nasdaq had been up as much as 0.5%) moderated steadily through the session as Warsh's remarks were digested as more hawkish than hoped.

## Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole speech — the week's defining event

In his first major address as Fed Chair, Warsh struck a notably hawkish tone on inflation, stating plainly that **"the Fed's predominant focus right now should be on prices"** — a direct signal that the central bank is not ready to declare victory on inflation. Current data: **PCE inflation at 3.7% year-over-year (headline), 3.3% core** — both comfortably above the Fed's 2% target. Warsh also noted he was "impressed by the overall performance of the economy," acknowledging resilience despite headwinds, but the market's takeaway skewed hawkish.

**Market reaction was a textbook "bear flattener":** short-term Treasury yields spiked roughly **8 basis points**, while longer-dated yields actually fell — markets repricing higher near-term rate expectations while moderating their longer-term outlook. The **10-year Treasury yield rose to roughly 4.72-4.73%**, up from Thursday's ~4.66%, with the market pricing only a **33.7% probability of a September rate cut** at last check ahead of the speech. Equities gave back their early gains as the hawkish framing sank in — the Nasdaq's initial +0.5% morning move fully reversed by the close.

## Other big single-stock stories

- **PayPal (PYPL) plunged over 11-14.6%** after **Advent International and Stripe abandoned their roughly $50-53 billion takeover pursuit** of the company — ending acquisition speculation abruptly and removing what had been a valuation floor for the stock.
- **Marvell Technology (MRVL) fell roughly 7-9%** despite beating Q2 expectations, as the company guided **Q3 margins to 57.5-58.5%, down sequentially from 58.9%** — margin compression overshadowed the top-line beat.
- **IREN Ltd. dropped about 7.7%** after Q4 results missed consensus revenue estimates.
- Markets continued digesting Nvidia's Wednesday beat and Thursday's follow-through rally (see `../260827/usa.md`) as the week's dominant AI-optimism backdrop, even as Friday's macro news took center stage.

## What to watch next week

1. **Follow-through on Warsh's hawkish tone** — whether the September rate-cut probability (33.7% pre-speech) falls further as markets fully digest his remarks.
2. **PayPal's next moves** without the Stripe/Advent takeover backstop — the stock's biggest single-day story of the week.
3. **Whether the Nvidia-driven AI rally (see this week's Taiwan, Korea, Japan coverage) can withstand a genuinely more hawkish Fed stance** heading into September.

## Bottom line

A week that opened with Nvidia's beat-and-raise and enterprise-software blowouts closed on a more sober note: Fed Chair Warsh's first major speech leaned hawkish on inflation, triggering a bear-flattener in yields and pulling US indices modestly lower into the weekend even as the broader AI-earnings story (Nvidia, Salesforce, CrowdStrike) remained intact. Add PayPal's abrupt loss of its takeover premium and Marvell's margin-guidance disappointment, and Friday reads as a genuine "reality check" session after two days of exuberance — worth watching for how it carries into next week's trading across the markets covered in this folder.
