# Korea Stock Market Analysis — August 28, 2026 (Friday)

## Headline

**KOSPI closed at 6,788.88, down 123.49 points (−1.79%)**, snapping back sharply from Thursday's 1.53% gain in a session that decisively broke from the week's AI-optimism narrative. **KOSDAQ closed slightly higher**, a notable divergence from the KOSPI's large-cap-driven decline. The proximate cause: a concentrated wave of foreign and institutional selling in Korea's two chip bellwethers, timed with heightened caution ahead of the **Jackson Hole Economic Symposium** (see `usa.md`).

## What drove the decline

- **Samsung Electronics fell 3.38% to 257,000 won.**
- **SK Hynix dropped 4.45% to 1,653,000 won.**
- Local coverage described a **"bombardment of foreign selling... concentrated on semiconductor bellwethers Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix"** — a sharp, name-specific move rather than a broad market rout, even though it dragged the whole index down given Samsung and SK Hynix's outsized index weights.
- **Foreign investors sold ₩1.76 trillion** worth of shares, and **institutions sold ₩284.1 billion**. **Retail investors absorbed ₩424.1 billion in net buying**, attempting — unsuccessfully — to stabilize the market against the much larger institutional/foreign selling wave.
- **The won appreciated to 1,372.5 per dollar**, reflecting broader global dollar weakness and market expectations for potential Fed rate cuts — an odd combination with the day's risk-off tone in equities, underscoring that the selloff was semiconductor-specific rather than a broad Korea risk-off move.

## Context: this looks like pre-Jackson Hole de-risking, not a trend change

The selling was explicitly framed around **"heightened wait-and-see sentiment ahead of the Jackson Hole Economic Symposium"** — in other words, investors trimming exposure to Korea's most AI-sensitive, highest-beta names (Samsung, SK Hynix) ahead of a known catalyst, rather than a reaction to any negative company-specific news. That reading is consistent with Thursday's own caution flag (`../260827/korea.md`), where rising margin debt and falling investor cash deposits already signaled a market vulnerable to exactly this kind of pullback.

## Bottom line

A sharp reversal that snapped Thursday's AI-optimism rally, driven almost entirely by concentrated foreign and institutional selling in Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix ahead of Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech. KOSDAQ's own resilience (closing slightly higher) shows this wasn't a broad Korea risk-off day — it was specifically a de-risking of the market's two largest, most AI-exposed names. Combined with Thursday's margin-debt warning sign, Friday's pullback looks like the first real confirmation that Korea's blistering month-long rally (KOSPI +22% over 20 trading days as of Thursday, see `../260827/README.md`) had become vulnerable to exactly this kind of profit-taking around a major macro catalyst.
