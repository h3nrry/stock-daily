## Indonesia (IHSG/JCI) — a quiet, mixed close after Thursday's surge

**IHSG closed at 6,518, down 3.63 points (−0.06%)** — essentially flat, consolidating after Thursday's strong 1.81% rebound. The session opened stronger (up to 6,535) before drifting down through the day. Sector performance was genuinely mixed: **energy, non-cyclical consumer, industrials, infrastructure, finance, technology, healthcare, property, and materials all declined**, while **cyclical consumer, finance, and transportation sectors gained** — a reminder that Thursday's broad, all-sector rally didn't carry through unchanged. Breadth was close to even (290 advancing, 348 declining, 325 unchanged) on **Rp14.9 trillion in turnover across 33.5 billion shares**. Standout gainers were smaller-cap names — Green Power Group (+34.38%), Sunson Textile Manufacture (+25%), and Bhineka Inovasi Ketahanan Energi (+24.52%) — rather than the large-cap banking names that led Thursday.

## Thailand (SET)

**SET Index closed at 1,588.22, down 12.48 points (−0.78%)**, under selling pressure concentrated in large-cap names — specifically **DELTA, AOT (Airports of Thailand), and ADVANC (AIS)** — described in coverage as the market "consolidat[ing] due to selling pressure in big caps." Some offsetting buying interest in commodity-linked names provided partial support. Analysts flagged continued volatility ahead as investors awaited Fed guidance from the Jackson Hole symposium.

## Bottom line

A quieter, more cautious end to the week across Southeast Asia, in line with the region-wide wait-and-see posture ahead of Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech (see `usa.md`). Indonesia essentially paused after Thursday's strong rebound, with sector leadership rotating away from Thursday's broad-based gains and toward smaller speculative names. Thailand's SET gave back nearly 0.8% on concentrated large-cap selling. None of the region showed the kind of decisive directional move seen in Korea (`korea.md`) or Taiwan (`taiwan.md`) today.
