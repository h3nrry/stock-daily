# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — August 28, 2026 (Friday)

## Headline

TAIEX (加權指數) surged **356.23 points (+0.77%) to close at 46,331.45**, a fresh record close, on turnover that broke back above **NT$1 trillion (NT$1.023T) for the first time since August 18**. The index opened up 95.61 points and briefly ran as high as 46,552.23 (+577.01) intraday before easing modestly into the close — still a genuinely strong, broad session rather than a fade like Thursday's. The rally was powered directly by the full market digestion of Nvidia's beat-and-raise (see `usa.md`), plus a large domestic policy tailwind and a fresh AI-optics narrative in Largan Precision.

## What drove the index

- **Largan Precision (大立光, 3008) extended its rally to a second consecutive limit-up**, touching an intraday record high of **NT$7,135 (+3.18%)** and breaking through the NT$7,000 level for the first time — continuing Thursday's CPO (co-packaged optics) supply-chain speculation (see `../260827/taiwan.md`). Market chatter centers on the potential for Largan to expand from smartphone camera optics into data-center Scale-Out-to-Scale-Up fiber array components if customer certification and mass production follow. Notably, **35 Taiwan-listed ETFs hold Largan shares, with 12 holding over 3% weighting** — the active-managed Yuanta Taiwan Growth Leaders (00992A) posted a two-day gain of 4.24% on the back of it, the strongest among optics-exposed funds.
- **MediaTek (聯發科, 2454) rose +3.1% to NT$3,985** and **Delta Electronics (台達電, 2308) jumped +3.39% to NT$1,830** — both riding the same AI-capex optimism.
- **Lite-On Technology (光寶科) surged +7.57%**, one of the day's standout percentage gainers.
- **Cathay Financial Holdings (國泰金) rose +2.93%**, giving financials a lift alongside tech.
- **TSMC (台積電, 2330) added a modest +0.41% to NT$2,420** — a real gain, but proportionally smaller than the broader market, consistent with money spreading out into AI-adjacent and optics names rather than concentrating in the index's single largest weight.
- **Weak spots: Unimicron (欣興, ABF substrates) fell −5.93%** and **Nan Ya Plastics (南亞) gave back −3.08%** after Thursday's limit-up — a clear case of profit-taking following an outsized prior-day move.
- **Policy tailwind**: passage of an **Autonomous Vehicle Regulation allocating NT$240 billion over six years** boosted drone- and defense-related names, and continued anticipation of Apple's September 9 autumn event (with foldable-iPhone speculation) supported Apple-supply-chain names including TSMC and Foxconn.

| Investor group | Net flow (Aug 28) |
|---|---|
| Foreign investors (外資) | **+NT$366.13B (買超)** — NT$1.058 trillion for the week |
| Investment trusts (投信) | **+NT$33.66B (買超)** |
| Dealers (自營商) | **+NT$179.34B (買超)**, including +NT$131.95B in hedging positions |

This is an enormous step up from Thursday's already-solid +NT$64.31B combined net buy — nearly a nine-fold increase, and among the largest single-day institutional buying figures in recent memory for this market. **Foreign investors also cut their TAIEX futures net-short position by 1,181 contracts to 83,655**, continuing the gradual short-covering trend flagged over the past several sessions. Breadth was more mixed than the headline suggests — 727 advancing issues against 1,049 declining — meaning the rally, while backed by real institutional capital, was concentrated in large caps and the AI/optics theme rather than broad-based.

## Bottom line

A genuinely powerful, record-closing session: TAIEX broke back above NT$1 trillion in turnover for the first time in ten sessions, and institutional investors — foreign money above all — poured in an extraordinary +NT$579.13B combined, nearly nine times Thursday's already-solid figure. Largan Precision's second consecutive limit-up (now above NT$7,000, a genuine record) is becoming one of the market's defining stories, backed by real ETF fund flows rather than pure speculation. A domestic policy tailwind (the NT$240B Autonomous Vehicle Regulation) and Apple-event anticipation added further support. With Friday's session capping a week that saw TSMC, MediaTek, and Largan all confirm the region's AI-optimism narrative with real capital, the open question heading into next week is whether Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole tone (see `usa.md`) introduces a macro headwind strong enough to interrupt the momentum.
