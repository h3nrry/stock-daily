# Korea Stock Market Analysis — September 4, 2026 (Friday)

## Headline

Korea's KOSPI closed out the week with a strong, semiconductor-driven rally — a marked contrast to Thursday's turbulent, nearly-4-percentage-point intraday swing (see `../260903/korea.md`). The index **rose 107.73 points (+1.64%) to close at 6,687.21**, approaching the psychologically important 6,700 level and touching an intraday high of **6,697.60**. The **KOSDAQ outperformed further, closing at 813.50, up 23.29 points (+2.95%)**. Both foreign and institutional investors returned as net buyers, both contributing on top of Samsung Electronics' and SK Hynix's continuing buyback support.

## What drove the rally

- **Record semiconductor exports provided the fundamental catalyst**: August semiconductor exports hit a **record $46.65 billion, up 209% year-over-year** — a genuinely strong data point that gave Friday's rally a fundamental, rather than purely technical or buyback-driven, foundation, distinct from Thursday's "no clear catalyst" institutional selloff.
- **Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix led the index directly**: **Samsung Electronics rose 2.60% to ₩256,500**, and **SK Hynix jumped 3.95% to ₩1,659,000** — both stocks accelerating their gains through the afternoon session as buying momentum built.
- **Foreign and institutional investors both turned net buyers**, a genuine reversal from Thursday's pattern where both categories sold into the midday collapse: intraday figures around 1:22pm showed **foreign investors net buying ₩356.5 billion** and **institutional investors net buying ₩1.052 trillion**.
- **Individual investors were the session's net sellers**, taking profits into the rally: **net selling of approximately ₩2.488 trillion**, continuing Thursday's pattern of retail turning seller rather than buyer during a directional move.
- **Financial stocks were a notable laggard** even as the broader index rallied — a genuine divergence, with financials showing weakness while semiconductor names drove the headline gain, a reversal from Thursday's session where financials (KB Financial +5.2%, Shinhan Financial +3.62%) were among the standout gainers.
- **Buyback support continued in the background**: Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix's ongoing buyback programs (72.6% and 73.0% of disclosed amounts remaining as of Sept 2) provided an additional floor beneath Friday's fundamentally-driven rally, the same mechanism that had been the primary support on Thursday's more turbulent session.

## Bottom line

A genuinely fundamentally-driven rally — record August semiconductor export data ($46.65B, +209% YoY) gave Friday's session a foundation that Thursday's turbulent, catalyst-less swing lacked, with both foreign and institutional investors returning as net buyers alongside Samsung Electronics' and SK Hynix's continuing buyback support. The clearest departure from Thursday's pattern is the return of foreign and institutional buying (rather than selling) and a KOSDAQ that is now outperforming for a second straight session — both genuine signs of improving underlying sentiment rather than the buyback-only support that characterized Thursday's calmer-than-it-looked close. Financials lagging behind the broader rally, even after Thursday's standout gains there, is worth watching as either a healthy rotation or an early signal of profit-taking spreading beyond individual investors.
