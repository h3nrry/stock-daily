# Japan Stock Market Analysis — September 4, 2026 (Friday)

## Headline

Japan's Nikkei 225 snapped its four-session losing streak with a sharp, decisive rebound. The index **closed at 65,020.94, up 806.46 points (+1.26%)** — its first gain in five trading days after a cumulative decline of roughly ¥2,200 over the prior four sessions (see `../260903/japan.md`). The rally was unusually concentrated: two stocks alone — **SoftBank Group and Advantest** — accounted for roughly **669 of the index's 806-point gain**, even as decliners on the Tokyo Prime Market (137 issues) outnumbered advancers (86 issues), underscoring how narrow this "broad-index" rally actually was beneath the headline number.

## What drove the rally

- **A direct read-through from Thursday's US rally**: US indices posted their best session in a month Thursday (Dow +1.18%, Nasdaq +1.51%) on Fed Governor Waller's dovish disinflation comments and falling Treasury yields (see `../260903/usa.md`) — that risk-on tone carried directly into Friday's Tokyo session, easing the risk-off pressure that had driven Japan's prior four down days.
- **SoftBank Group (9984) surged approximately 11.8%**, alone contributing an estimated **¥473–589** of the index's point gain (sources vary slightly on the exact figure) — by far the single largest driver of Friday's rally.
- **Advantest (6857) rose sharply** (+¥810), the session's second-largest index contributor; combined with SoftBank, the two names alone pushed the index up by roughly ¥669 — meaning the other 223 stocks in the index contributed comparatively little net to the day's ¥806 gain.
- **AI and semiconductor names led broadly** beyond just Advantest, continuing the theme that has driven much of this year's Nikkei volatility — both on the way down during sector-specific scares and on the way up during recoveries like today's.
- **Sector performance was genuinely mixed despite the strong headline number**: information/communications, securities and commodity futures trading, and non-ferrous metals rose, while **mining, shipping, and electric power fell** — a reminder that Friday's gain was concentrated in specific mega-cap names and sectors rather than a uniform market-wide advance.

## Money flow — 外国人 (foreign investors)

Same-day foreign-investor flow data via JPX's weekly investor-type statistics was not available at writing time (JPX publishes on a weekly, not daily, cadence — the same limitation flagged in Thursday's file). Friday's price action — a narrow, mega-cap-concentrated rally with more decliners than advancers even as the index surged — is consistent with a session driven by concentrated buying in a handful of AI/semiconductor names rather than a broad-based foreign-investor return to the market; this remains a read on sentiment rather than a confirmed flow figure.

## Bottom line

A five-day-high, decisive rebound on the headline number, but a genuinely narrow one underneath — SoftBank Group and Advantest alone accounted for roughly 83% of the index's 806-point gain, and more Prime Market stocks fell than rose even as the Nikkei posted its best session in weeks. The rally directly tracked Thursday's strong US session on Fed Governor Waller's dovish comments, ending a four-day losing streak that had cost the index nearly ¥2,200. Whether this move broadens beyond two mega-cap names into next week — or proves to be another single-day bounce like the ones that punctuated August's volatility — is the clearest thing to watch heading into the weekend.
