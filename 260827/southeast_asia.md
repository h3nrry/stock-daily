# Southeast Asia Stock Market Analysis — August 27, 2026 (Thursday)

## Singapore (STI)

**Straits Times Index closed at 5,684.12, down about 37 points (−0.65%)** from Wednesday's 5,721.59, continuing the modest slippage flagged the previous day (STI slipped in early trade too, opening down 0.51%). No single dominant catalyst stood out in coverage — a quiet pullback in an otherwise Nvidia-optimism-driven regional session.

## Indonesia (IHSG/JCI) — a sharp rebound, demonstration notwithstanding

**IHSG closed at 6,521.75, up 116.06 points (+1.81%)**, a strong rebound from Wednesday's 1.48% decline — and notably, this gain came on the very day (Aug 27) that Jakarta's planned demonstration had been expected to weigh on sentiment (see `../260826/southeast_asia.md`). **Healthcare led with a 6.26% sector surge**, followed by non-essential consumer goods (+3.53%), utilities (+3.03%), and property (+2.69%) — genuinely broad-based, with all sectors positive and 578 stocks advancing against just 103 declining. Trading value was relatively modest (Rp13.51 trillion), suggesting the rally reflected relief/positioning rather than a surge of fresh capital. Top single-stock contributor: PT Sejahteraraya Anugrahjaya (SRAJ), alongside continued strength in banking names Bank Central Asia (BBCA) and Bank Mandiri (BMRI).

## Malaysia (FBM KLCI)

FBM KLCI eased at midday to around **1,742.40 (−0.35%)** from Wednesday's 1,748.54 close, giving back a slice of Wednesday's two-day-slide rebound. The pullback came **despite** a broadly positive regional Nvidia read-through — analysts specifically flagged that "Nvidia's strong results and upbeat outlook are expected to provide a positive read-through to local technology counters" — but financial services and plantation names (Maybank, Public Bank) dragged, while utilities, telecoms, media, and industrial products/services held up better. *(Exact closing figures were not available among sources checked as of writing; the midday reading is used as the best available reference.)*

## Thailand (SET)

**SET Index closed at 1,600.70, essentially flat (−0.08%)** from Wednesday's 1,601.92 — the calmest of the four Southeast Asian markets covered here today.

## Bottom line

A genuinely split day for the region: Indonesia posted the standout gain, rebounding sharply and broadly even with a planned demonstration on the calendar, while Singapore and Malaysia both eased modestly and Thailand stayed essentially flat. Unlike Taiwan, Korea, and Japan, none of Southeast Asia's markets showed a strong same-day Nvidia-optimism bid — this region continues to trade more on its own local catalysts (Indonesia's political-event relief, Malaysia's sector-specific rotation) than on US tech-earnings spillover.
