# USA Stock Market Analysis — August 27, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

US indices rallied broadly as the market completed its verdict on Nvidia's Wednesday-evening earnings beat, and enterprise-software names delivered their own blowout results overnight. **S&P 500 closed at 7,730.99 (+0.72%)**, the **Nasdaq Composite surged to 26,541.35 (+1.57%)**, and the **Dow Jones added a comparatively modest 53,569.44 (+0.20%)** — the wide gap between Nasdaq and Dow underscoring just how concentrated the day's gains were in technology. Nvidia itself rose **8.74%**, its 13th consecutive quarter of topping guidance, on 106% revenue growth and 128% EPS growth, while still trading at just ~25x forward earnings by one measure cited in coverage.

## Enterprise software stole a share of the spotlight from Nvidia

- **Salesforce (CRM) surged 22.58%** after beating expectations and announcing a partnership with Anthropic to launch "Claudeforce" — one of the single biggest stock moves of the week.
- **CrowdStrike (CRWD) jumped 20.50%** on strong earnings.
- **Veeva Systems (VEEV) rose 15.20%.**
- Together with Nvidia's beat, these results "may have temporarily eased concerns about AI disruption to the SaaS sector" — a real second storyline behind the day's tech-led rally, not just an Nvidia echo.
- **Loser of the day: Hormel Foods (HRL), down 10.25%** after missing earnings expectations.

## What else drove today

- **Sector split:** enterprise software and semiconductors surged; consumer discretionary and real estate lagged.
- **Jackson Hole positioning began in earnest**: Friday's Kevin Warsh keynote — his first major speech as Fed Chair — was already the dominant forward-looking topic in coverage, with markets bracing for signals on the Fed's inflation-fighting stance (see `README.md` for Friday's actual outcome).
- **Regional read-through**: the same "optimism ahead of/following Nvidia" dynamic that lifted Taiwan (`taiwan.md`) and Japan (`japan.md`) earlier in the Asian session carried into the US cash session as a genuine, earnings-confirmed rally rather than pure anticipation.

The rally keeps riding the same dollar-weakness and crypto-friendly-legislation optimism flagged repeatedly this month.

## What to watch next

1. **Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote, Friday** — his first as Fed Chair, and the week's single most important catalyst for yields, the dollar, gold, and bitcoin.
2. **PayPal and Marvell earnings reaction** — both reported after Thursday's close and moved sharply overnight (see Friday's `usa.md` for the fallout).
3. **Whether Thursday's ETF rotation (out of SPY/gold, into QQQ/SOXX) persists** or reverses once the Nvidia and software earnings news is fully digested.

## Bottom line

A genuinely strong, earnings-confirmed rally — not just anticipation, but a real verdict on Nvidia's beat-and-raise plus a surprise second engine from enterprise software (Salesforce, CrowdStrike, Veeva) that broadened the AI trade beyond chips. The Nasdaq's 1.57% gain against the Dow's 0.20% shows how concentrated the enthusiasm was in tech specifically. ETF flows confirmed the rotation was real money, not just index drift. With that uncertainty now resolved, Friday's Jackson Hole speech is the last major swing factor of the week — and, as it turned out, delivered its own hawkish surprise (see the next day's `usa.md`).
