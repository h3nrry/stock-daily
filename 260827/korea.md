# Korea Stock Market Analysis — August 27, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

**KOSPI closed at 6,912.37, up 104.16 points (+1.53%)**, and **KOSDAQ rose to 837.65 (+1.30%)** — Korea's strongest session of the week, riding the same Nvidia-driven AI/semiconductor enthusiasm sweeping the region (see `taiwan.md`, `japan.md`). But underneath the headline gain, local coverage flagged a genuinely concerning supply-demand signal worth tracking.

## A rally with a warning sign underneath

Retail investors were net sellers of **2.25 trillion won** on the main board even as the index rallied — the kind of divergence that doesn't derail a single session but is worth watching. More pointedly, **investor deposits (the cash sitting ready to buy stocks) fell to 98.92 trillion won, below the 100-trillion-won level for the first time in nine sessions**, while **margin loan balances climbed to 33.10 trillion won for a seventh consecutive day**. One local report called this combination — falling cash cushion, rising leveraged debt — "a textbook warning sign on supply and demand." It's not a call that the rally is over, but a signal that more of the recent gains are being financed with borrowed money rather than fresh cash, which historically makes a market more fragile to any negative surprise.

## Bottom line

A strong headline day for KOSPI and KOSDAQ that fits the region's broader Nvidia-earnings enthusiasm, but with a structural undercurrent — falling investor cash deposits and rising margin debt — that reads as a genuine caution flag rather than noise. Combined with Friday's Jackson Hole speech as the next major catalyst (see `usa.md`), and Friday's actual KOSPI reaction (a sharp reversal — see `../260828/korea.md`), Thursday's rally looks in hindsight like it may have been running further ahead of its own cash support than the price action alone suggested.
