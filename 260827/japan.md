# Japan Stock Market Analysis — August 27, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

**Nikkei 225 closed at 66,123.50, down 132.96 points (−0.20%)**, a session local coverage described as "lacking clear direction" — advancing and declining issues were nearly evenly balanced, and the modest headline decline masked a session where one stock did almost all the damage. **TOPIX** continued its own separate winning streak (see `usa.md`'s Friday coverage for the streak's later extension), reflecting a broader market that held up better than the headline index suggests.

## What drove the (modest) decline

- **Advantest (アドバンテスト)**, the Nvidia-linked chip-testing equipment maker, was the single largest drag on the index, subtracting roughly **202.74 yen** from the Nikkei's point value on its own — more than the entire index decline. The stock's partial recovery during the session (losses had been as wide as 380 yen at 10:00 a.m.) helped the Nikkei claw back much of its early slide.
- **Kioxia Holdings led gainers**, contributing +56.55 yen, with additional support from **Fujikura** and **TDK**.
- Breadth was close to even, consistent with a market genuinely undecided rather than one-sidedly bearish — a contrast to the sharp, single-narrative moves seen in Taiwan (`taiwan.md`) and the US (`usa.md`) the same day.

## Context: ahead of Nvidia's full read-through

Thursday's session in Tokyo closed before the fullest read-through of Nvidia's Wednesday-evening beat-and-raise had time to fully propagate through Japan's own chip-equipment and testing names — Advantest's intraday round-trip (down as much as 380 yen, recovering to a ~133 yen net Nikkei drag) suggests the market was still working out exactly how to price the results specific to Japan's semiconductor supply chain, ahead of Friday's more decisive move (see `../260828/japan.md`).

## Bottom line

A quiet, technically indecisive session dominated by a single stock's intraday round-trip: Advantest's early-session slide and partial recovery explains essentially the entire Nikkei's modest decline, while breadth stayed close to even and TOPIX kept extending its own longer winning streak. With Nvidia's results now fully known and Friday's Jackson Hole speech the next major catalyst, Thursday reads as a pause for digestion rather than a real change in trend.
