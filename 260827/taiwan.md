# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — August 27, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

TAIEX (加權指數) gained **142.60 points (+0.31%) to close at 45,975.22**, a session that told two very different stories depending on when you looked. The index opened sharply higher on Nvidia's blowout earnings and guidance (see `usa.md`), surging as much as **550+ points to briefly breach 46,400** in early trade — but selling pressure emerged mid-session and trimmed most of those gains by the close. Turnover came in at **NT$924.68B**, still elevated versus the month's average. A senior analyst (Tsai Ming-han) framed the pattern as a normal "consolidation before advancement" after a big earnings beat, with volatility expected as the market digests positioning changes.

## What drove the index

- **Largan Precision (大立光, 3008) hit limit-up (+10%), closing at a new record of roughly NT$6,915** — the standout story of the day, and the start of a two-session limit-up run (see `260828/taiwan.md` for the follow-through). Speculation centered on Largan's potential entry into the **CPO (co-packaged optics) supply chain**, where a move from data-center Scale-Out to Scale-Up architecture could open a new, non-smartphone revenue stream in fiber array units if customer certification and mass production follow.
- **Plastics (塑膠類) was the day's strongest sector, +7.99%**, led by Nan Ya Plastics (南亞) and Chung Kuo Insurance-linked petrochemical names hitting daily limits (NT$227.50 and NT$14.30 respectively) — read-through from continued strength in semiconductor-adjacent materials.
- **OTC/small caps (櫃買指数) rose 1.19%**, outpacing the main board.
- **TSMC (台積電, 2330) slipped modestly, −0.21% to NT$2,410** — a mild pullback even as the broader AI trade found new legs elsewhere, consistent with the day's "gains outside the index heavyweight" pattern.
- **Financials (+0.20%) and Electronics (+0.16%)** both opened strong but faded as the session wore on, in line with the broader intraday give-back.
- **Weak spots:** Steel (−1.15%) and Tourism (−0.74%) were the session's laggards.

Foreign investors were the dominant force, continuing to build positions in active ETFs and memory-related names according to local coverage — a second straight day of institutional support following Wednesday's +NT$59.39B combined net buy (see `../260826/taiwan.md`). Two consecutive sessions of meaningful institutional buying, even as the index itself gave back most of an early 500+ point spike, suggests real capital is accumulating on dips rather than chasing the opening pop.

## Bottom line

A session of two personalities: an explosive open on Nvidia's earnings that briefly pushed TAIEX above 46,400, followed by profit-taking that left the index up a comparatively modest 0.31% by the close. Underneath that muted headline number, two things stand out — a genuine new bullish narrative in Largan Precision (CPO optics, not just smartphone camera demand) that produced a record-high limit-up, and a second consecutive day of solid net institutional buying (+NT$64.31B) that suggests foreign and dealer money continues to accumulate through the volatility. With Friday bringing Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech (see `usa.md`), the next real test for Taiwan is whether Thursday's institutional support carries through a macro-driven session.
