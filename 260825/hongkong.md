# Hong Kong Stock Market Analysis — August 25, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

**Hang Seng Index** closed essentially flat, down about **6 points to 25,511** (Investing.com's live feed shows a very similar **25,465, −0.21%**), on turnover of **HK$254.08B**. The **Hang Seng TECH Index** fell a marginal **0.1% to 4,588**. A quiet, directionless session — regional chip-stock strength (tracking the Korea/Taiwan rebound covered in `korea.md` and `taiwan.md`) provided support, but wasn't enough to push Hong Kong's own benchmark into clearly positive territory.

## What drove the (lack of) movement

- **Chip-related stocks tracking South Korea's rebound** helped support regional sentiment, offsetting some of the drag from other sectors.
- **Investors stayed cautious ahead of Nvidia's Wednesday earnings and Fed Chair Warsh's Friday Jackson Hole speech** — the same two catalysts dominating every market covered in this folder this week.
- Monday's Alibaba-driven weakness (an ~8% drop on a large share placement, which dragged Tencent, Meituan, Xiaomi, and JD.com lower in sympathy — see Monday's write-up) does not appear to have extended into a second down day at the index level, consistent with that being a company-specific, not sector-wide, story.

## Bottom line

Hong Kong took a breather today: essentially flat at the index level, with chip-sector strength from the region offsetting lingering caution ahead of this week's two big catalysts. This is a calmer read than Monday's Alibaba-driven 1.3%+ drop, and — as with Taiwan and Korea — looks more like consolidation ahead of Wednesday/Friday than a continuation of Monday's tech-driven weakness. Worth watching whether Alibaba and the broader mainland-tech complex stabilize further once Nvidia's results are out.
