# Japan Stock Market Analysis — August 25, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

**Nikkei 225** closed at **65,090–65,198** (sources vary slightly on the exact print), down roughly **0.10–0.5%**, a modest pullback led by chip-related names even as the broader **Topix** index edged up **+0.06% to 4,075.83** — a split verdict that points to index-weight mechanics (heavy semiconductor weighting in the Nikkei 225) rather than a broad market decline. Shipping stocks were the bright spot, up **2.91%** on Middle East-tension-driven value rotation.

## What pushed the index down

- **Chip-related stocks led the decline**: Advantest −3.33%, Tokyo Electron −1.03%, Kioxia −1.37% — all trading cautiously ahead of Wednesday's Nvidia earnings.
- **Samsung Electronics' weakness spillover**: Monday's disappointing Samsung shareholder-return announcement (see `korea.md`) continued to weigh on regional chip sentiment into Tuesday's session, even as Korea's own market rebounded intraday.
- Broader caution ahead of Nvidia: "the market is cautious ahead of Nvidia earnings due on Wednesday," per market commentary, with investors watching for signs that AI-capex growth is slowing.

## What rallied

- **Shipping stocks, +2.91%** — investors rotating into value/defensive names amid ongoing Middle East tensions (Iran conflict, Strait of Hormuz shipping-disruption risk), the same freight-strength theme that lifted Taiwan's shipping names earlier this week (`taiwan.md`).
- **Topix outperformed the Nikkei** (+0.06% vs. Nikkei's decline), underscoring that today's weakness was concentrated in large-cap chip names rather than broad-based.

## Money flow (資金流向) — foreign investor activity

Fresh weekly JPX (Japan Exchange Group) foreign-investor flow data for the week including August 25 was not available among the sources checked for this write-up — JPX publishes this on a weekly lag, and the most recent published figure (covering the prior week, per Monday's report) showed foreign investors net-selling ¥198.8 billion, ending a two-week buying streak, but explicitly framed by local coverage as a pause rather than a reversal in an intact bullish stance. Readers wanting the latest weekly print should check the JPX source linked below directly.

## Currency and BOJ context

The **yen (USD/JPY)** traded around **159.36**, up modestly (+0.16%) on the day — a small reversal within its recent strengthening trend, which has been driven by rising odds (~82% as of Monday) of a Bank of Japan rate hike in September. A stronger yen is typically a headwind for Japanese exporters' reported earnings, a dynamic worth watching alongside the chip-sector weakness above.

## Bottom line

A quiet, mixed session: the headline Nikkei 225 dipped modestly on chip-sector weakness tied to pre-Nvidia caution and Samsung-shock spillover from Korea, while the broader Topix stayed positive and shipping stocks rallied on Middle East-driven freight strength. This looks like the same "index-weight mechanics, not broad risk-off" pattern seen across the region this week (compare Taiwan's and Korea's write-ups) — worth watching whether Wednesday's Nvidia print resolves the chip-sector caution in either direction.
