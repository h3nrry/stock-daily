# Southeast Asia Stock Market Analysis — August 25, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Singapore (STI)

Same-day (Aug 25) closing data for the **Straits Times Index** was not available among the sources checked for this write-up as of writing. The most recent confirmed close — **Monday, Aug 24: 5,680.46, −0.15%** — remains the latest reference point; readers wanting today's exact close should check the sources below directly. STI has been trading near record territory for weeks (+33.45% YoY as of Monday's report), supported by strength in DBS, OCBC, and UOB.

## Indonesia (IHSG / JCI)

Same-day (Aug 25) closing data for **IHSG** was also not available among the sources checked. The most recent confirmed close — **Monday, Aug 24: 6,501.67, −0.37%** — followed broader Asian market weakness, with healthcare (−2.3%), real estate (−1.27%), and financials (−0.86%) the weakest sectors, partially offset by raw materials (+1.21%). Indonesia's current-account deficit widened to a record $12.5B (3.3% of GDP) in Q2 2026, an ongoing domestic concern layered on top of regional sentiment swings.

## Malaysia (FBM KLCI)

**FBM KLCI** closed at **1,736.33**, down just **0.15 points (−0.01%)** — essentially flat, with losers outnumbering gainers 714 to 482. Trading stayed subdued ahead of a Malaysian public holiday and in anticipation of Friday's Jackson Hole symposium. Analyst Thong Pak Leng noted that "stronger earnings results from index heavyweights could provide the catalyst for the next upward move" — the market is in a holding pattern waiting for a fresh trigger.

## Thailand (SET)

**SET Index** closed at **1,596.01**, down **5.04 points (−0.31%)**, on trading value of THB 59.75B. The energy sector fell on softer crude prices, and electronics names (led by **DELTA**) declined on rising-US-bond-yield concerns, while banking, food, and power-plant sectors attracted value-focused rotation away from the weaker sectors. Upcoming catalysts flagged by local commentary: US PCE data, Nvidia's earnings, and Friday's Jackson Hole symposium — the same three events dominating every market in this folder this week.
