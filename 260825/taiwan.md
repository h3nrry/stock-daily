# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — August 25, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

TAIEX (加權指數) closed at **45,169.46**, up **407.14 points (+0.91%)**, bouncing back above the 45,000 level (and back above its quarterly moving average / 季線, per local coverage) a day after Monday's dip, on **turnover of NT$696.06B (~US$21.88B)** — meaningfully heavier volume than Monday's four-month low. It was a genuinely volatile session under the surface: the index swung across a **959-point intraday range (44,210.31 to 45,169.46)** before TSMC's late-session strength pulled it up to a closing high, per Cnyes (鉅亨網) local coverage. The rally tracked a broader Asia-wide semiconductor rebound (Korea's KOSPI clawed back from an early-session rout — see `korea.md`) after Monday's Samsung-driven chip scare proved to be company-specific rather than sector-wide.

**Market internals (盤面觀察):** the Electronics sub-index (電子股指數) rose 0.82% and accounted for **74.67% of total market turnover** — the AI/semiconductor complex is still doing almost all of the market's trading volume, even on a day the bigger percentage mover was financials. The Financials sub-index (金融保險股) rose 2.31% on a much smaller 2.95% share of turnover. The OTC/Emerging board (櫃買指数, TPEx) rose 0.86% to 389.41, tracking the main board higher. The New Taiwan dollar weakened slightly to **31.889 per USD (−3.5 分/cents)** on forex turnover of US$685M — a small depreciation that runs counter to the broader global dollar-weakness theme covered in `README.md`, worth noting as a local nuance rather than reading too much into a single day.

## What pushed the index up

- **TSMC / 台積電 (2330)** rose 1.05% to NT$2,400 (+NT$25), recovering Monday's session-low weakness late in the day and, given its ~40%+ index weight, doing most of the heavy lifting on the closing bounce.
- **Financials were the standout sector, +2.31%**, led by **Fubon Financial Holding / 富邦金 (2881)**, which jumped **7.20%** after reporting strong investment returns — the single biggest sector story of the day.
- **Petrochemicals (塑化股) rebounded 1.82%**, a sharp reversal from Monday's 5.45% sector rout, with **Nan Ya Plastics / 南亞 (1303)** up 4.72%.
- **Memory (記憶體) names extended their rally**: Nanya Technology / 南亞科 (2408) +1.80%, Winbond Electronics / 華邦電 (2344) +1.13%, continuing the DRAM/AI-server-demand story that started Monday.
- **Passive components were split**: Walsin Technology / 華新科 (2492) +1.13% to NT$268, while sector heavyweight **Yageo / 國巨 (2327)** fell **1.27% to NT$544** — a reminder that even within a rallying sub-sector, not every name participated.
- Robotics-related machinery makers also posted gains, consistent with continued rotation into industrial automation names.

## PCB / copper-clad laminate (CCL) — a fresh sector story, from local Taiwan coverage

Local Chinese-language coverage (Cnyes / 鉅亨網, UDN / 聯合新聞網) surfaced a sector story largely absent from English-language wire coverage today: a sharp rally in **PCB upstream materials — copper-clad laminate (CCL) / 覆銅板** — driven by AI-server demand and a company-specific capacity/earnings catalyst at **Elite Material / 聯茂 (6213)**.

- **Elite Material / 聯茂 (6213)** hit **limit-up (漲停), +9.84% to NT$530**, on heavy volume (~25,000 lots), after (1) announcing a major capacity expansion — its Wuxi plant adding ~2.85M sheets/year and its Thailand plant adding ~600K sheets/year by 2028, lifting total planned CCL capacity from 5.45M sheets this year to 8.9M sheets by 2029 — and (2) reporting blowout Q2 results: revenue of NT$11.53B (+26.1% QoQ, +29.9% YoY, a quarterly record), after-tax net profit of NT$1.279B (**+104% YoY**), and EPS of NT$3.52. First-half after-tax profit of NT$1.594B already exceeds all of last year's NT$1.51B.
- The rally spread through the CCL supply chain: **Taiflex / 台虹 (8039)** also hit **limit-up, +10.0% to NT$291.50**; **Thintech Materials-KY / 騰輝電子-KY (6672)** rose **4.44% to NT$270.50**; **Elite Taiyo / 台燿 (6274)** turned from red to green, **+2.06% to NT$1,485**.
- The market narrative: mid-to-high-end CCL prices are expected to keep rising through the second half, driven by demand from AI servers, network switches, low-earth-orbit satellites, and memory modules — a demand story, not a one-off.
- Separately, the **substrate "Big Three" (載板三雄)** — **Nanya PCB / 南電 (8046)** (+7%+ intraday), **Unimicron / 欣興 (3037)** (+4%+ intraday), and **Kinsus / 景碩 (3189)** (+3%+ intraday) — all rallied strongly even while carrying TWSE's **"disposition stock" (處置股)** status, a trading-restriction flag TWSE applies to stocks with abnormally high recent volatility. That they kept rising despite the restriction is itself a signal of how much conviction there currently is behind the AI-substrate demand story.

## Money flow (資金流向) — is money leaving Taiwan or coming in?

**The full three-institution (三大法人) breakdown tells a more nuanced story than the headline index gain suggests** — despite TAIEX closing up 0.91%, all three institutional categories were net sellers on balance today:

| Investor group | Net flow (Aug 25) |
|---|---|
| Foreign investors (外資) | **−NT$787M (賣超)** (net sell) |
| Investment trusts (投信) | **−NT$4.61B (賣超)** (net sell — a reversal from Monday's net buy) |
| Dealers (自營商) | +NT$60M (買超) (roughly flat, small net buy) |
| **Three institutions combined (三大法人)** | **≈ −NT$5.34B (net sell)** |

This is a genuinely interesting divergence: the index rose on broad-based retail/other buying and a late-session TSMC bounce, even as institutional investors were, on net, distinctly *not* the ones pushing it higher — investment trusts in particular flipped from Monday's net buying to today's net selling. Foreign selling itself shrank to a fraction of Monday's NT$15.75B outflow (a rounding error next to a NT$696B turnover day), so the picture is "foreign investors stepped to the sidelines, domestic institutions turned cautious too, and the rally was driven by other participants" rather than a clean "institutions bought the recovery" story.

**The bigger trend remains unchanged from Monday**: foreign investors have net-sold a cumulative ~NT$2.28 trillion (~US$70.7B) year-to-date 2026 and still hold roughly 48.7% of total market cap — a large, gradual trim of an overweight position, not a stampede out. TAIEX continues to trade at a stretched valuation (~29.75x forward earnings as of Monday, about two standard deviations above its 10-year average), so today's bounce doesn't change the underlying "be selective, not passive" read from institutional strategists covered in Monday's write-up.

## Bottom line

A volatile but ultimately positive day: TAIEX swung across a 959-point range before a late TSMC-led push carried it to a 407-point (+0.91%) close, recovering all of Monday's index-level loss and then some. Under the headline number, the real story is broader than "TSMC bounced" — financials (Fubon), a petrochemical reversal, continued memory strength, and a fresh, well-documented PCB/copper-clad-laminate rally (Elite Material's limit-up on a genuine earnings beat, spreading into the substrate "Big Three") all point to real sector-level conviction, especially around AI-server-linked materials and components. The one note of caution: despite the index gain, all three institutional investor categories (foreign, investment trust, dealer) were net sellers on balance today — a divergence between the index level and institutional positioning worth watching, alongside the unchanged year-to-date foreign-selling trend and stretched ~29.75x forward valuation. With Nvidia's earnings Wednesday and Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech Friday still ahead, today's strength looks more like a relief rally with real sector substance behind it than a fully resolved trend — worth watching whether the CCL/substrate names and financials can hold their gains once those two catalysts pass.
