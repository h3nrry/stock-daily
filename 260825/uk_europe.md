# UK & Europe Stock Market Analysis — August 25, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

Europe extended its recent winning streak today. **FTSE 100** rose **0.16% to 10,871.17**, its **sixth consecutive session of gains**. **CAC 40** climbed **+0.36% to 8,483.42**. **DAX** was roughly flat (−0.11% to ~26,107), pausing after a strong recent run. The pan-European **STOXX 600** rose **+0.48% to 657.38**, a fresh high for the month. Europe remains the calmest, steadiest region in this week's global session — a contrast with the chip-sector-driven swings in Asia and the US.

## What's driving the gains

- **Melrose Industries** led the FTSE 100, surging over 8% after announcing a $100 million claims program tied to its GKN Aerospace California facility incident, with operations expected to restart by September 28.
- **Defense and industrial stocks (+~1.2%)** and **housebuilders** also contributed to FTSE strength.
- **Mining stocks** continued providing ongoing support, a theme that's run throughout the region's winning streak.
- **Less severe US-Iran sanctions than markets had feared, plus easing crude oil prices**, helped reduce inflationary-pressure concerns across the region — a genuinely supportive macro backdrop rather than single-stock noise.

## Bottom line

Europe's calm, steady grind higher continued for a sixth straight session on the FTSE, with the STOXX 600 hitting a fresh high for the month. Unlike Asia and the US, where today's story was dominated by a chip-sector rebound after Monday's Samsung-driven scare, Europe's gains look driven by genuine macro relief (softer-than-feared Iran sanctions, easing oil) plus idiosyncratic stock stories (Melrose). With Nvidia's earnings Wednesday and Jackson Hole Friday still ahead, Europe's relative calm this week is worth watching — it could either continue decoupling from the AI-driven volatility elsewhere, or catch up with it if either catalyst surprises markets.
