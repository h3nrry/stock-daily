# Japan Stock Market Analysis — September 1, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

**Nikkei 225 closed slightly lower, down roughly 96 points (~−0.14%)**, a second consecutive small decline as sellers and buyers traded control through a choppy, directionless session ("売り買い交錯でもみ合う展開" — a mixed, range-bound pattern per local reporting). This follows Monday's near-complete recovery from a 1,500-point intraday plunge (see `../260831/japan.md`), suggesting the market is consolidating rather than trending decisively in either direction as it enters September.

*Note: the exact closing level was not independently confirmed via a fully-accessible source at writing time (two Japanese-language outlets were blocked to automated fetching); the ~96-point decline is drawn from headline text ("日経平均終値96円安") and should be treated as approximate pending confirmation.*

## What drove the market

- **US market weakness carrying over**: Japanese markets opened under pressure from continued softness on Wall Street tied to the Iran/oil-driven risk-off tone (see `../usa.md`).
- **10-year JGB yield approaching 3%**, a level not seen in some time, adding a domestic rates headwind alongside the global one — see "Nikkei Edges Lower as 10-Year Yield Hits 3%" in sources below.
- **A genuinely mixed, low-conviction session**: multiple local reports characterized the day as range-bound with no clear directional catalyst, consistent with a market digesting Monday's volatility rather than reacting to fresh news.

## Bottom line

A quiet, consolidative session after Monday's dramatic intraday round trip — Nikkei edged modestly lower in a session local commentary described as directionless, with rising JGB yields (nearing 3%) adding a domestic macro headwind alongside the still-unresolved Iran/oil situation weighing on global sentiment. Not a high-conviction day either way; worth watching whether the 10-year JGB yield's approach to 3% becomes a more active market theme into the rest of September.
