# Korea Stock Market Analysis — September 1, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

**KOSPI closed at 6,835.80, up 0.23%**, holding the 6,800 level for a second straight session, while **KOSDAQ fell 1.56% to 821.25** — a clear large-cap/small-cap divergence. Notably, the KOSPI gain came despite **individuals, foreign investors, and institutions all net-selling** — the index was held up almost entirely by **"other corporations" net-buying ₩1,688.8 billion**, attributed primarily to **Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix share buybacks**.

## Money flow (資금 흐름)

| Investor group | Net flow (Sept 1, KOSPI) |
|---|---|
| Individuals (개인) | **−₩472.4B (net sell)** |
| Foreign investors (외국인) | **−₩541.1B (net sell)** |
| Institutions (기관) | **−₩675.3B (net sell)** |
| Other corporations (기타법인) | **+₩1,688.8B (net buy)** |

This is a genuinely unusual flow pattern: every conventional investor category (retail, foreign, institutional) was a net seller, and the index still rose — entirely on the strength of corporate buyback activity concentrated in Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. That's a structurally different kind of support than organic buying demand, and worth watching for whether it persists once buyback programs run their course.

## What drove the divergence

**KOSDAQ's 1.56% decline** while KOSPI rose points to a rotation out of smaller-cap, higher-beta names and into (or at least away from selling in) the large-cap buyback beneficiaries — consistent with a market where genuine risk appetite (reflected better in KOSDAQ) was actually negative, masked at the headline KOSPI level by mechanical buyback support.

## Bottom line

A headline KOSPI gain that conceals a fundamentally cautious underlying market: individuals, foreigners, and institutions were all net sellers, and the index was held up almost entirely by Samsung Electronics/SK Hynix buyback-related "other corporation" buying. KOSDAQ's steeper decline is probably the more honest read on genuine investor risk appetite today. Worth flagging as a session where the headline number and the underlying flow story point in different directions — a useful reminder to always check the composition of a KOSPI move, not just its sign.
