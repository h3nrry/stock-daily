# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 1, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

**TAIEX (加權指數) surged 820.25 points (+1.78%) to close at 46,948.72**, on trading value of roughly **NT$1.08–1.13 trillion** (sources vary slightly: NT$1.081T per PTT/market recap, NT$1.129T per Cnyes), just shy of the psychologically important 47,000 level. The rally was overwhelmingly driven by one story: **NVIDIA's first-ever direct investment in a Taiwan-listed company** — a **US$3.5 billion (~NT$110.6 billion) purchase of MediaTek convertible bonds**, announced August 31 after Taiwan's market close. **MediaTek (聯發科, 2454) opened the session gapped straight to its daily limit-up of NT$4,315 (+9.94%) and stayed locked there ("鎖死") for the entire day**, with a buy-order queue exceeding **26,000 lots** that never cleared. *(Correction: an earlier version of this file understated both the TAIEX close and the MediaTek/Nvidia story — this is the corrected write-up.)*

## The NVIDIA–MediaTek deal — Taiwan's first direct Nvidia investment

This is the single most important Taiwan story of the day, and arguably of the week:

- **Deal structure**: MediaTek issued **US$3.9 billion in five-year, zero-coupon exchangeable convertible bonds (ECBs)**, to be listed on the Singapore Exchange on September 8. **NVIDIA subscribed to 17,500 units — roughly 90% of the entire offering** — making it overwhelmingly an NVIDIA-funded deal rather than a broadly-syndicated one.
- **Conversion terms**: initial conversion price of **NT$4,513.75/share** (a 15% premium to the pre-announcement closing price), with **maximum equity dilution capped at 1.67%** if fully converted — a genuinely small dilution for the scale of the capital and strategic signal involved.
- **Strategic rationale**: this elevates the two companies' relationship from a technical collaboration (they already work together on edge AI, PCs, and automotive) into a **capital alliance extending into cloud AI infrastructure**. MediaTek will adopt **NVIDIA's NVLink Fusion platform and the newly-announced NVHBM technology** to design **custom XPU/ASIC chips for hyperscale cloud operators and AI model developers** — positioning MediaTek as a second major supplier of AI-datacenter silicon alongside NVIDIA's own GPUs, rather than a competitor to them.
- **Jensen Huang's framing**: NVIDIA's CEO described this as **"a long-term product roadmap spanning ten years,"** and pushed back directly on online skepticism that this was circular AI financing (i.e., NVIDIA investing in a customer that then buys more NVIDIA product), citing MediaTek's own strong profitability and NVIDIA's confidence in the investment's return.
- **Market's read**: local coverage explicitly framed this as **"Taiwan's first case"** ("台灣首例") of NVIDIA taking a quasi-equity stake in a Taiwan-listed firm, and described MediaTek's ten-year product roadmap being revealed as part of the announcement — a materially bigger deal, structurally, than a typical supply agreement.
- **Skepticism worth noting**: online retail-investor commentary flagged two real concerns worth tracking — potential **equity dilution** from bond conversion (small at 1.67% max, but real), and whether this reflects a broader pattern of **circular capital deployment across AI companies** (NVIDIA investing in firms that are also its customers/partners) that has drawn scrutiny industry-wide in 2026.

## What else drove the index

- **Broad semiconductor-sector participation** beyond MediaTek alone: silicon wafers, compound semiconductors, and packaging-materials names all showed strong momentum, consistent with the market reading the NVIDIA-MediaTek deal as bullish for Taiwan's broader AI-supply-chain positioning, not just MediaTek in isolation.
- **TSMC (台積電, 2330)** also rose (up roughly NT$20–35 depending on source, to around NT$2,415–2,425) on improved global semiconductor sentiment and ahead of SEMICON Taiwan 2026 (opening September 2) — a real contributor, but a distinctly secondary one to MediaTek's limit-up on this particular day.
- **OTC market (櫃買指數) also rallied, +2.26% to 410.77**, confirming the optimism was not confined to the main board.

## Bottom line

A genuinely landmark day for Taiwan's market, not just a strong one: NVIDIA's first-ever direct investment in a Taiwan-listed company — $3.5 billion into MediaTek, structured as convertible bonds with a 10-year AI-datacenter roadmap attached — sent MediaTek straight to a locked limit-up and pulled the broader semiconductor supply chain up with it, driving TAIEX to +1.78% and within sight of 47,000. This reads as a genuine strategic-alliance signal (NVLink Fusion-based custom XPUs for hyperscale cloud) rather than a one-off financial transaction, though the modest net institutional buying (NT$5.61B) alongside such an outsized index move is worth remembering — a single locked-limit-up stock can move the index mechanically without matching capital-flow conviction across the broader market. Worth tracking closely into the bond's September 8 Singapore listing and any further deal details.
