# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 1, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

**TAIEX (加權指數) surged 641.75 points (+1.39%) to close at 46,770.22**, a strong start to September on trading value of **NT$327.0 billion**. The index opened up 253.48 points and extended gains through the session, fully reversing Monday's dip (see `../260831/taiwan.md`) and pushing back toward the record territory seen in late August. **TSMC (台積電, 2330) rose NT$20 to NT$2,425**, and semiconductor sentiment broadly improved alongside a 0.57% gain in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index overnight.

## What drove the index

- **SEMICON Taiwan 2026, opening September 2**, provided a positive forward-looking catalyst for the semiconductor sector heading into the session — supply-chain names positioned ahead of the show's announcements and customer meetings.
- **Overnight Philly Semiconductor Index strength (+0.57%)** gave Taiwan's chip-heavy index a constructive read-through despite the weekend's Iran-driven oil spike still weighing on broader US sentiment (see `../usa.md`).
- **TSMC's gain (+NT$20 to NT$2,425)** was a primary driver given its outsized index weight, consistent with the sector-wide semiconductor recovery.

## Bottom line

A strong reversal from Monday's volatility: TAIEX added +1.39% to open September, powered by TSMC and broad semiconductor-sector optimism ahead of Wednesday's SEMICON Taiwan 2026 show and a constructive overnight read from the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index. This is a genuinely encouraging start to the month, though it's worth watching whether the underlying institutional money-flow picture (foreign/investment-trust/dealer) confirms this as broad-based buying once same-day data is published, versus a narrower TSMC-led move.
