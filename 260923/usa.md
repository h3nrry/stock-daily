# USA Stock Market Analysis — September 23, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

**Strong US economic data triggered a bond selloff.** September's S&P Global flash PMIs beat forecasts by a wide margin (composite **58.4 vs 55.3 expected**; services 58.7 vs 55.8; manufacturing 57.0 vs 53.7), showing business activity growing at its **fastest pace in five years** while input costs rose. The **10-year Treasury yield jumped about 11–12bp to ~5.08–5.11%, its highest level since 2007**, and traders raised the odds of an **October Fed hike to roughly 70%**. Stocks fell across the board: **S&P 500 −0.75% to 7,706.03, Dow −0.68% to 51,511.59, Nasdaq Composite −1.13% to 26,936.04, Russell 2000 −1.77% to 2,838.66.** The Nasdaq's two-day run of record closes ended.

## What drove the session

- **"Good news is bad news."** The PMI beat was the fourth straight month of accelerating activity, and the release showed rising cost pressure. AP summed it up: "Pressure from the U.S. bond market hit a new level on Wall Street after a surprisingly strong report on the economy raised worries about inflation."
- **The Fed side added to it.** Governor **Michael Barr** said "further policy adjustments are likely to be needed" to fight sticky inflation (Yahoo Finance), and hawkish comments from **Musalem (St. Louis) and Goolsbee (Chicago)** pushed December hike odds to about 90% per USAGOLD's read of futures pricing. The Fed hiked to 3.75–4.00% on Sept 16 (see `../260916/usa.md`).
- **Housing hurt:** the 30-year mortgage rate rose to **7.12%, a two-year-plus high** (Motley Fool). **KB Home** slipped despite beating earnings.
- **Worries about AI agents hit financials and travel.** LPL Financial, Charles Schwab, JPMorgan, Booking Holdings, Expedia and Arthur J. Gallagher fell on fears that AI agents will disrupt their businesses (Yahoo Finance). The same theme likely explains Expedia's −22.59% drop and Schwab's weakness on Sept 22, which `../260922/usa.md` had flagged as unexplained.
- **Megacaps split:** Alphabet −3.80%, Amazon −2.24%, while Meta +1.02% and Microsoft +0.52% (Saxo).
- **Winners:** **Micron +5.00% to $1,096.16** ahead of its **Sept 30** earnings (ad-hoc-news/dpa-AFX); **cybersecurity** (CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks) on their role in AI safety; **quantum computing** (IonQ, D-Wave); **Cracker Barrel +3%** on an earnings beat; **energy**, as oil rose ~4%.
- **Oil rose sharply** (Brent +3.86% to $103.08) after Iran's president vowed never to "surrender" to the US. That added to inflation worries on the same day as the PMI surprise.
- **Trump–Xi:** Xi arrived for his first Washington visit in 11 years, bringing a smaller-than-expected business delegation. The summit was set for Thursday (see `../260924/usa.md`).

## Bottom line

Wednesday's selloff was driven by **stronger-than-expected data, not a growth scare**: PMIs at five-year highs sent the 10-year yield to its highest level since 2007 (~5.1%), raised October hike odds to about 70%, and pulled the S&P 500 down 0.75%, the Nasdaq 1.13% and the Russell 2000 1.77%. Oil's ~4% jump made the inflation worry worse. Micron and cybersecurity stocks were the notable exceptions.
