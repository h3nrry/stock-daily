# Hong Kong Stock Market Analysis — September 23, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

The **Hang Seng Index fell 253.63 points (−1.01%) to 24,834.12**, ending a three-day winning streak, and the **Hang Seng Tech Index fell 1.33%**. Selling was concentrated in **large-cap internet stocks and the newly listed AI large-language-model (大模型) stocks**: **Zhipu (智谱) fell nearly 12%**, Alibaba (阿里巴巴) fell more than 4%, Xiaomi (小米集团) more than 3% and Tencent (腾讯控股) more than 2%. One expert quoted by Sing Tao Headline summed up the mood before the Trump–Xi summit: "市场对习特会无期望" ("the market has no expectations for the Xi–Trump meeting"). **PCB and mainland property stocks rose against the trend**, and **southbound Stock Connect buyers stayed net buyers (+HK$3.449 billion)**.

## What drove the session

- **The AI-model and internet stocks gave back Tuesday's gains.** Tencent had led the index on Tuesday (+5.02%, see `../260922/hongkong.md`). On Wednesday the same group fell: Alibaba −4%+, Xiaomi −3%+, Tencent −2%+. Among recently listed LLM names, **Zhipu (智谱) fell ~12%** and **MiniMax (MINIMAX-W) fell ~4%**.
- **PCB stocks rebounded.** **Kingboard Laminates (建滔积层板, 1888) rose more than 11%**, Kingboard Holdings (建滔集团, 0148) more than 5%, and **Victory Giant Technology (胜宏科技)** more than 3%. That extended the AI-server substrate/PCB theme running in Taiwan (Gold Circuit limit-up) and Korea (see `taiwan.md`, `korea.md`), after the sector's sharp drop in Hong Kong on Tuesday.
- **Mainland property stocks jumped.** **Ronshine China (融信中国) rose more than 22%** and **China Vanke (万科企业) about 4%**. This was a speculative rotation, not a change in fundamentals.
- **Cautious going into the summit.** Xi Jinping was arriving in Washington for his first visit in 11 years, with a smaller business delegation than expected (Yahoo Finance). Hong Kong traders reduced exposure to China tech before the meeting.

It also sold into Kingboard Laminates' 11% jump. The two Stock Connect channels traded Zhipu in opposite directions, which reflects fast-moving speculative money in the AI-model names more than a clear view.

## Bottom line

Hong Kong **fell 1.01% to 24,834.12** as the AI-model and large-cap internet stocks that drove Tuesday's gain reversed ahead of the Trump–Xi summit. PCB and mainland property stocks rose, and southbound money kept buying (+HK$3.449bn) and added to Tencent and Alibaba on the dip. Overnight, the US 10-year yield hit a 2007 high, and Hong Kong fell again the next day (see `../260924/hongkong.md`).
