# Korea Stock Market Analysis — September 23, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

The **KOSPI rose 63.01 points (+0.90%) to 7,080.92** on the **last trading day before the Chuseok (추석) holiday**. KRX is closed Thursday–Friday, Sept 24–25, and reopens Monday, Sept 28. Korean media described the session as **"전강후약" (strong early, weaker late)**: the index rose to about **7,110** in the morning, then gave back part of the gain as traders stayed cautious before the holiday (연휴 앞둔 관망세). The KOSDAQ rose 10.10 points (+1.21%) to **844.48**. **Samsung Electronics (삼성전자) rose 3.07% to ₩285,000** and drove most of the gain. **Individuals and foreigners were both net sellers**, and the buying came from institutions and "other corporations" (기타법인).

## What drove the session

- **Samsung-group heavyweights led.** Samsung Electronics +3.07% (₩285,000), **Samsung Electronics preferred (삼성전자우) +4.02%**, **SK Square (SK스퀘어) +4.32%**, and **SK Hynix (SK하이닉스) +1.25%** to ₩1,863,000. This was a partial recovery after Tuesday, when both chip giants gave back steep morning gains (see `../260922/korea.md`).
- **Defensives and financials fell.** Samsung Biologics (삼성바이오로직스) −1.58%, Hyundai Motor (현대차) about −1.4% to −1.5% depending on the snapshot, and KB Financial (KB금융) about −1.1% to −1.2%. Money moved toward chips and away from other large caps, similar to the US bank-to-tech rotation on Sept 22.
- **Holiday caution capped the gain.** With two sessions off, the Trump–Xi summit on Sept 24, and US flash PMIs due that evening, traders cut positions into the close. That caution paid off: the US 10-year yield later jumped to 5.11% and the Nasdaq fell 1.13%. Korea will absorb that move when it reopens on Sept 28.

Money Today's headline ("기관·기타법인 매수세") and Dailian's headline ("개미·외인 '팔자'") agree on the direction. **Data note:** a summarized fetch of Newspim's closing wrap assigned the same three figures to the wrong investor groups (it showed individuals as net buyers). That contradicts both headlines and was discarded. This is the same unit/label mix-up seen with Korean sources on Sept 21. Money Today's 15:44 snapshot showed institutions at +₩317.4bn, which matches closely.

**Read:** foreigners have now sold on most sessions this month, and individuals sold heavily before the holiday. Institutional and corporate buying was enough to lift the index, but the rally is narrow and mostly Samsung. KOSDAQ: individuals and institutions bought, foreigners sold (−486억원).

**Won:** the won closed at **₩1,358.40 per dollar**, little changed on the day.

## Bottom line

Korea went into Chuseok **up 0.90% at 7,080.92**, a second straight close above 7,000, led by Samsung Electronics (+3.07%). Underneath, individuals sold ₩1.44 trillion and foreigners ₩511 billion, and only institutional and corporate buying kept the index up. With KRX closed Sept 24–25, Korean stocks miss the global rate shock that started Wednesday night US time and Thursday's Trump–Xi summit. **Monday Sept 28 will have to price all of it at once, two days before Micron's Sept 30 earnings.**
