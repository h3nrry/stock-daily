# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 23, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

The **TAIEX rose 357.12 points (+0.75%) to close at a record 48,157.29**, its sixth straight gain and the first close above 48,000 (站穩48K). The index opened higher and swung through the session (開高震盪), reaching an intraday high of **48,341.56 (+541.39)** before settling off the top. Turnover was about **NT$856 billion** (成交量8,560億元), lower than Tuesday's NT$1.02 trillion. **TSMC (台積電, 2330) led, +NT$40 (+1.63%) to NT$2,500**, reclaiming the 2,500 level after Tuesday's red close. Breadth was weak again, though: **close to 1,000 listed and OTC stocks fell** (上市櫃下跌家數高達近千家), and the OTC index slipped 0.34% to 413.76. So the record was carried by a few heavyweights, not the broad market. **Institutions were net buyers for a fifth straight day (+NT$38.922 billion)**, almost all of it from foreign investors (+NT$37.313 billion).

## What drove the session

- **TSMC and memory led the gains.** After Tuesday's 801-point intraday reversal (see `../260922/taiwan.md`), TSMC rebounded to NT$2,500 (+1.63%), and memory names rallied together (記憶體族群齊揚): **Nanya Technology (南亞科, 2408) +3.62% to NT$515**, **Winbond (華邦電, 2344) +0.88%**, **Powerchip (力積電, 6770) up nearly 2%**. MediaTek (聯發科, 2454) added NT$5 to NT$5,185, and Hon Hai (鴻海, 2317) rose 1.19% to NT$256.
- **The PCB/substrate theme kept going.** **Gold Circuit Electronics (金像電, 2368) hit limit-up at NT$1,140** (收漲停), and **Compeq (華通, 2313)** and **Unimicron (欣興, 3037)** each rose more than 3%, extending the AI-server PCB/ABF rally that also ran in Korea (see `korea.md`) and in Hong Kong's Kingboard Laminates (see `hongkong.md`).
- **AUO fell hard.** After two straight limit-up sessions on the reported Intel Micro-LED partnership, **AUO (友達, 2409) fell more than 5%** and Innolux (群創, 3481) fell nearly 2%. UMC (聯電, 2303) closed flat.
- **Lower oil helped sentiment.** One local analyst (鐘崑禎 of 摩爾證券投顧, quoted by China Times) said oil "officially broke below $100" and argued that if it stays there, the TAIEX could reach 50,000 faster. This is one analyst's view, not this project's forecast. Brent in fact **rebounded +3.86% to $103.08** later that day in US trading (see `README.md`), so that support may not last.
- **The Trump–Xi summit came next.** Thursday was the last session before a four-day holiday (Sept 25 Mid-Autumn Festival, Sept 28 Teachers' Day) and the first day of the Washington summit.

## Bottom line

Taiwan set another record (**48,157.29, +0.75%**), led by TSMC's rebound to NT$2,500, a memory-sector rally and more limit-up PCB names, and foreign investors bought for a fifth straight day. But nearly 1,000 stocks fell, investment trusts sold, and the US 10-year yield jumped to a 2007 high after Taiwan's close. That set up a harder test for Thursday's last pre-holiday session (see `../260924/taiwan.md`).
