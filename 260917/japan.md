# Japan Stock Market Analysis — September 17, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

The **Nikkei 225 rose 213.25 points (+0.33%) to close at 64,136.25**, a second straight advance as Tokyo got its first chance to trade on the Fed's actual FOMC decision (25bp hike to 3.75%-4.00%, hawkish dot plot — see `../260916/usa.md`). The **TOPIX outpaced the Nikkei by a wide margin, rising 32.47 points (+0.80%) to 4,094.19** — a genuine divergence from the Nikkei's more modest gain, driven by a rotation within the session from semiconductors into value stocks. Nikkei's own coverage summed up the day as **"波乱なし" FOMCに安堵** ("no surprise" from the FOMC brought relief), even as rising AI-regulation discourse weighed on sentiment at the margin.

## What drove the session

- **Relief that the Fed delivered a "no surprise" outcome was the session's dominant theme.** Nikkei's own headline framed Thursday's gain explicitly around FOMC relief, even though the dot plot itself (16 of 18 officials seeing at least one more 2026 hike) was the hawkish surprise that hit US equities Wednesday — Japanese investors appear to have treated the hike itself, now confirmed and behind the market, as the bigger event to clear.
- **A genuine within-session sector rotation**: Kabutan's own headline described the session as **"続伸、半導体失速もバリュー株が買われる"** (continued gains, semiconductors lost steam but value stocks were bought) — a rotation away from the AI/chip trade that had driven Wednesday's broad rebound (see `../260916/japan.md`) and into more defensive/value-oriented names instead.
- **The TOPIX's stronger 0.80% gain versus the Nikkei's 0.33%** is itself evidence of this rotation: a value-led, broader-market advance tends to lift the TOPIX (which is more heavily weighted toward financials and industrials) more than the Nikkei (more semiconductor/tech-weighted), the mirror image of Wednesday's closely-matched 0.69%/0.61% pair.
- **Rising AI-regulation discourse was flagged as a headwind** ("AI規制論は重荷に" — AI regulation debate weighs) in the Nikkei's own coverage, a new and somewhat different concern from the chip-partnership-driven optimism that dominated Wednesday's session — worth tracking as a potential drag on the semiconductor names that had led Wednesday's rally.
- **US index futures were firmly higher heading into the Tokyo close** (Dow +0.7%, S&P +0.8%, Nasdaq +1.1% premarket per Wall Street's own Thursday session — see `../usa.md`), providing a supportive overnight backdrop even as Tokyo's own session showed signs of profit-taking in the specific names that had led Wednesday's advance.

## Money flow — 外国人 (foreign investors)

Same-day foreign-investor flow data was not available at writing time — the Japan Exchange Group (JPX) publishes investor-type statistics weekly rather than daily, the same limitation this project has flagged repeatedly. The session's own internal rotation (semiconductors losing steam, value stocks being bought, TOPIX outpacing the Nikkei) is a more informative real-time signal than the lagged weekly JPX data would be for Thursday specifically, but it remains inference from price action rather than a confirmed flow figure.

## Bottom line

Thursday's Nikkei gain (+0.33%) undersells the more interesting story underneath: the TOPIX's stronger 0.80% advance reflects a genuine sector rotation away from the semiconductor names that drove Wednesday's rebound and into value stocks, even as the broader market took relief from the Fed's FOMC decision landing with "no surprise" from Tokyo's perspective (despite the hawkish dot plot's surprise for US markets specifically). Rising AI-regulation discourse is a new, distinct headwind worth tracking separately from the macro/rate story that has dominated this project's coverage all week. With this session marking Tokyo's first full trading day after the actual Fed decision, Friday's session will be the one to watch for whether Thursday's value rotation persists or the chip trade reasserts itself.
