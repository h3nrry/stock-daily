# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 17, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

The **TAIEX rose 439.1 points (+0.95%) to close at 46,288.0**, a second straight session of gains as Taiwan's market got its first chance to react to Wednesday night's FOMC outcome (a 25bp hike to 3.75%-4.00% paired with a hawkish dot plot — see `../260916/usa.md`) and did so calmly, extending Wednesday's rebound rather than selling off on the hawkish surprise. Trading value came in at roughly **NT$819.4 billion**, and market breadth was strongly positive (**832 advancers vs. 273 decliners**, 25 hitting daily limit-up against just 1 limit-down) — a genuinely broad-based session, a contrast with Wednesday's narrower, foreign-selling-clouded rebound. **TSMC (台積電, 2330) rose NT$45 (+1.89%) to close at NT$2,425**, continuing to fill its ex-dividend gap from Wednesday's session.

## What drove the session

- **Taiwan absorbed the Fed's hawkish dot plot without a "sell the news" reaction.** Despite 16 of 18 FOMC officials now projecting at least one more 2026 hike, Thursday's session showed the opposite of Wednesday's mild caution — investors bought broadly across the board rather than de-risking, a signal that the initial FOMC-week jitters have largely passed.
- **TSMC's continued strength (+1.89% to NT$2,425) mechanically lifted the index further**, building on Wednesday's flat, ex-dividend-day close and reinforcing that underlying demand for the stock remains resilient even as global chip sentiment has been more mixed (see `japan.md`, where semiconductors "stalled" the same day).
- **UMC (聯電) rose 3.87%**, among the session's stronger large-cap gainers, while **plastics (塑膠) surged 4.82%** and **shipping (航運) gained 1.93%** — a genuinely broad sector rally rather than a single-theme (chip-only) move.
- **Electronic components (−2.03%) and optoelectronics (−0.87%) were the session's notable laggards**, alongside **MediaTek (聯發科, 2454), down 0.66%,** and **Delta Electronics (台達電), down 1.46%** — a reminder that Thursday's broad gain wasn't universal even within the tech-heavy index.
- **All three institutional investor categories were net buyers on the same day**, a clean reversal from Wednesday's pattern (when the index rose despite ≈NT$213.8 billion in net institutional selling) — see money-flow detail below.

This is consistent with the broad market breadth (832 advancers vs. 273 decliners) and suggests Thursday's gain reflects a genuine return of institutional conviction rather than the short-covering/domestic-only dynamic flagged in Wednesday's session.

## Bottom line

Thursday's +0.95% TAIEX gain was a genuinely more encouraging session than Wednesday's: broad market breadth (832 advancers vs. 273 decliners), all three institutional investor categories turning net buyers (+NT$21.477 billion combined) after Wednesday's ≈NT$213.8 billion net-sell overhang, and continued strength in TSMC (+1.89%) and UMC (+3.87%) across a genuinely multi-sector rally (plastics, shipping) rather than a chip-only move. Same-day ETF data for 0050/00878/00919 was available today, a welcome break from this project's persistent same-day-data gap for Taiwan ETFs. Taken together, Taiwan's market absorbed the Fed's hawkish dot-plot surprise calmly — a contrast worth watching against Friday's session for confirmation that foreign investors' return to buying is durable rather than a one-day bounce.
