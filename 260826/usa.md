# USA Stock Market Analysis — August 26, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

US indices closed **little changed to modestly lower** — Dow **53,463.88 (−0.21%)**, S&P 500 **7,675.70 (−0.02%, essentially flat)**, Nasdaq Composite **26,130.20 (−0.08%)** — a quiet, wait-and-see session ahead of the single biggest catalyst of the week: **Nvidia's earnings, released after the closing bell**. July's core PCE inflation reading came in at **+3.3% year-over-year**, matching expectations and unchanged from the prior month — a non-event for Fed policy expectations heading into Friday's Jackson Hole speech. Elsewhere, single-stock news dominated: **Meta** rose after agreeing to pay up to **$16.7 billion** to settle a social-media-addiction lawsuit, removing a legal overhang; **Abercrombie & Fitch surged 35%** on raised guidance; and **Salesforce jumped 13%** on better-than-expected guidance.

## Nvidia earnings — the day's real story, released after the close

Nvidia reported **Q2 FY2027 revenue of $96.2 billion**, beating the $91.9B consensus, with **adjusted EPS of $2.22** (vs. $2.08 expected) and an adjusted **gross margin of 75%**. The company guided **Q3 FY2027 revenue to $108 billion (±2%)**, and CEO Jensen Huang forecast **70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028** — far above analyst estimates — telling the earnings call "the only regret that I have is that I didn't invest more and sooner." Despite the beat-and-raise, **shares initially slipped about 1.5% in after-hours trading**, as some investors focused on margin pressure from rising memory costs rather than the top-line strength. That reaction reversed sharply by Thursday's session, with NVDA opening **up roughly 6–7%** as the "beat and raise" read won out overnight — a reminder that the immediate after-hours tape and the next day's verdict can tell very different stories. This is the single most important input for every market covered in this folder over the next 24 hours.

## What else drove today

- **Chip-sector positioning ahead of the print**: US chip names traded cautiously into the close, mirroring the same "wait for Nvidia" posture seen across Taiwan (`taiwan.md`) and Korea (`korea.md`) today, where index gains were explicitly attributed to "optimism that Nvidia's results and guidance will be encouraging."
- **Meta's $16.7B settlement**: resolves a long-running social-media-addiction trial overhang; the stock rebounded on the news even as it remains, per one commentator, "one hated stock."
- **Abercrombie & Fitch +35%** and **Salesforce +13%**: both jumped on raised/beat guidance, the clearest single-stock winners of the session.
- **Oil eased further**: WTI and Brent both drifted modestly lower, extending this week's pullback from the highs (see Oil section in `README.md`) as Iran–Oman talks over a Strait of Hormuz shipping corridor reduced supply-disruption fears.
- **Jackson Hole, Friday**: still two days out — Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's keynote remains the week's other major catalyst, alongside GDP and durable-goods data still to come.

## What to watch next

1. **Nvidia's after-hours reversal, Thursday** — the stock's initial ~1.5% after-hours dip flipped to a ~6–7% opening gain; whether that holds through the full session is the first real test of whether markets believe the 70%-fiscal-2028-growth guide.
2. **Jackson Hole, Friday** — Fed Chair Warsh's keynote is now the week's last major catalyst, with the dollar-weakness/gold/bitcoin trade the biggest cross-asset bet riding on its tone.
3. **GDP and durable-goods data** — still due before Friday, additional inputs into the market's Fed read.
4. **ETF flow data for Aug 25–26** — worth checking whether Monday's rotation into gold and out of large-cap/semiconductor funds continued or reversed around the Nvidia print.

## Bottom line

A quiet, largely uneventful cash session gave way to the week's real catalyst after the bell: Nvidia's beat-and-raise, an initial ~1.5% after-hours stumble on margin concerns, and a sharp overnight reversal to a ~6–7% gain as the growth guidance won out. Alongside that, Meta cleared a multi-billion-dollar legal overhang and two retail/software names (Abercrombie, Salesforce) posted the day's biggest single-stock pops on guidance beats. With Jackson Hole still two days out, today reads as the market clearing its most important near-term uncertainty (Nvidia) while saving its verdict on Fed policy for Friday.
