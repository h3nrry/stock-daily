# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — August 26, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

TAIEX (加權指數) surged **663.16 points (+1.47%) to close at 45,832.62** — its best single day in weeks — recovering all of "moving averages" (均線) per local coverage, on **turnover of NT$804.25B (~US$25.3B)**, up from NT$724.26B the prior day. The index opened lower, dipped as far as **44,925.84** in early trade, then rallied hard through the session to an intraday high of **45,878.39**, a swing of roughly 950 points. The move tracked the same regional theme as Korea's KOSPI (+0.97%, see `korea.md`) and Japan's Nikkei (+0.5-0.6%, see `japan.md`): **broad optimism heading into Nvidia's after-the-close earnings report**, with one analyst quoted as saying "the gains enjoyed by tech stocks reflected optimism about AI development on expectations that Nvidia's results and guidance will be encouraging." Breadth was strongly positive, with 1,415 listed companies advancing. The OTC/Emerging board (櫃買指數, TPEx) rose 1.6% to 395.66.

## What pushed the index up

- **AI/semiconductor and AI-adjacent names led broadly.** **Global Unichip (創意電子)** hit **limit-up (漲停), +10% to NT$6,125**. **AIchip Technologies (世芯-KY)** rose **+2.46% to NT$3,960**. **MediaTek (聯發科, 2454)** jumped **+5.62% to NT$3,945**, more than half a daily limit. **TSMC (台積電, 2330)**, after opening weak, turned positive to close **+0.62% at NT$2,415** — a modest gain individually, but given its roughly 40%+ index weight, an important confirmation that the broader rally had real conviction behind it.
- **AI-substrate and optics names extended their multi-week run.** **Largan Precision (大立光, camera lenses/CPO products)** hit **limit-up, +10% to NT$6,290**. **Kinsus Interconnect Technology (景碩, ABF substrate materials)** rose **+5.51% to NT$881**. **Unimicron Technology (欣興, PCB/ABF substrates)** rose **+5.94% to NT$1,160**.
- **Petrochemicals also hit limit-up in places**: **Nan Ya Plastics (南亞)** and **Grand Pacific Petrochemical (大東)** both reportedly touched the daily limit, extending the sector's recovery from earlier-week weakness.

## What pulled back — container shipping

The one clear group of losers was **container shipping (貨櫃三雄)**: **Yang Ming Marine Transport (陽明, 2609) fell −9.43% to NT$57.60**, **Evergreen Marine (長榮, 2603) fell roughly −6% to NT$231**, and **Wan Hai Lines (萬海, 2615)** also fell sharply. This reads as **profit-taking after an extraordinary multi-week run** — local coverage in the days prior had noted Wan Hai's share price had gained roughly 7x its combined January–July advance in a single month, with all three shipping names hitting fresh 2026 highs — rather than a fresh negative catalyst on freight rates or fundamentals. Financials were also modestly softer (−0.32%), a mirror image of Tuesday's leadership by Fubon Financial.

## Money flow (資金流向) — a decisive turn back to buying

**All three institutional investor categories (三大法人) flipped to net buying today**, a sharp reversal from Tuesday's net-sell position:

| Investor group | Net flow (Aug 26) |
|---|---|
| Foreign investors (外資, including mainland-linked) | **+NT$36.60B (買超)** (net buy) |
| Dealers (自營商) | **+NT$17.93B (買超)** (net buy) |
| Investment trusts (投信) | **+NT$4.86B (買超)** (net buy) |
| **Three institutions combined (三大法人)** | **+NT$59.39B (net buy)** |

This is a meaningful shift from Tuesday's ≈−NT$5.34B combined net sell (see `README.md` for yesterday's context) and from the broader pattern of gradual foreign de-risking flagged over the past two weeks. In the futures market, foreign investors also trimmed their net-short position in TAIEX futures from 85,380 to 83,654 contracts — still a substantial net-short book (over 80,000 contracts), but a second straight session of reduced bearish positioning. Read together: today's price action wasn't just retail enthusiasm ahead of Nvidia — real institutional capital, foreign money in particular, came back into the market.

## Container shipping — a name to watch for the opposite reason

**Yang Ming (陽明, 2609)**, **Evergreen (長榮, 2603)**, and **Wan Hai (萬海, 2615)** all fell sharply after a huge multi-week run (Wan Hai alone reportedly up several multiples of its January–July gain in the past month). Today's declines of 6–9%+ look like overdue profit-taking rather than a reversal in freight-rate fundamentals, but the size of the pullback — the sharpest in any sector today — is worth tracking over the next few sessions to see whether it stabilizes or extends.

## Bottom line

A powerful, broad-based rally: TAIEX swung nearly 950 points intraday before closing up 663 points (+1.47%), its strongest session in weeks, on turnover 11% higher than Tuesday's. Unlike Tuesday's TSMC-led bounce with institutions still net selling underneath, today's advance was confirmed by a genuine institutional-flow reversal — foreign, dealer, and investment-trust money all turned net buyers, and foreign futures short positioning eased for a second day. AI-adjacent names (Global Unichip, Largan, MediaTek, the substrate makers) led, while container shipping gave back some of its extraordinary multi-week gains in what looks like profit-taking rather than a fundamental turn. All of this happened **before** Nvidia's results landed after Taiwan's close — meaning today's rally was built entirely on anticipation. Thursday's session in Taiwan will be the first real market test of whether Nvidia's beat-and-raise (see `usa.md`) justifies the optimism, or whether today's gains were, in the end, a "buy the rumor" move.
