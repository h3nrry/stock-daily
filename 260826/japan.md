# Japan Stock Market Analysis — August 26, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

**Nikkei 225** closed higher for a second straight session, up roughly **0.5–0.6%** to around **66,220–66,260** (sources converge in this range; see note on sourcing below), while the **Topix** also advanced. The gain reversed an intraday slide of as much as **1.4%** earlier in the session. Volatility stayed elevated — the Nikkei Volatility Index jumped **8.11% to 30.41** — reflecting genuine two-way uncertainty ahead of Nvidia's after-the-US-close earnings report, the same catalyst driving today's cautious-but-positive tone across Taiwan (`taiwan.md`) and Korea (`korea.md`).

## What drove the reversal

- **Chip-related stocks tracked South Korea's rebound**, per Reuters/MarketScreener coverage — the same "Samsung buyback plan lifts regional chip sentiment" theme covered in `korea.md` today. **Advantest**, a major Nvidia supplier, still fell **−3.33%**, a reminder that even within a rallying market, pre-earnings positioning in the most Nvidia-exposed names stayed cautious.
- **Real estate, banking, and textile sectors led gains** broadly, per Investing.com's sector breakdown for the session.
- **Notable individual gainers**: Mitsubishi Materials (+5.95%), Tokyo Electric Power (+3.96%), Furukawa Electric (+4.30%), Shift Inc. (+4.32%), Mitsubishi Chemical Group (+3.62%).
- **Notable decliners**: SUMCO (−3.90%), Kawasaki Kisen Kaisha (−3.36%), Nippon Yusen (−2.48%) — shipping names giving back some of their recent Middle-East-tension-driven strength, echoing the same profit-taking dynamic hitting Taiwan's container shippers today (`taiwan.md`).
- **Market breadth was strongly positive**: 2,129 advancing issues vs. 1,357 decliners on the Tokyo Stock Exchange.

## A note on today's closing figure

Sources diverge on the exact Nikkei 225 close for today by roughly 800–900 points (a range of about 66,217 to 67,085 depending on the feed), while converging closely on the **percentage move (+0.5% to +0.64%)**. This write-up uses the percentage-based range as the more reliable figure; readers wanting the single official settled close should check the Nikkei Indexes source linked below directly.

## Currency and BOJ context

**USD/JPY** traded around **159.27**, essentially flat (+0.03%) on the day, holding in the same tight range it has occupied since Monday. The yen's recent strengthening trend — tied to rising odds (~82% as of earlier this week) of a September Bank of Japan rate hike — has stalled for the moment, a small tailwind for exporter earnings sentiment that complements today's index gain.

## Bottom line

A choppy but ultimately positive session: the Nikkei swung from an intraday loss of over 1% to a modest gain, tracking the same regional "cautious optimism ahead of Nvidia" theme playing out in Taiwan and Korea today. Real estate, banking, and select industrials led; Nvidia-exposed chip-equipment names like Advantest stayed defensive into the print; and container-shipping names gave back some recent gains. With Nvidia's results now released (see `usa.md`) and showing a beat-and-raise with an initial after-hours stumble that reversed sharply by Thursday's US open, Thursday's Tokyo session is the first real test of how Japan's chip-equipment and AI-adjacent names react to the actual numbers rather than the anticipation.
