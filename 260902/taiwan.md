# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 2, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

Taiwan's rally ran straight into the same Iran-driven oil shock hitting every other market this week. The **TAIEX plunged 784 points (−1.67%) to close at 46,164.72**, giving back the bulk of Tuesday's NVIDIA/MediaTek-driven gains (see `../260901/taiwan.md`) as **turnover hit roughly NT$920.3 billion**. Three institutional investors turned decisively negative, net-selling a combined **NT$115.03 billion (三大法人合計賣超1,150.34億元)** — a sharp reversal from Tuesday's modest net buying.

## What drove the drop

- **The same US-Iran conflict driving every market this week**: renewed fighting pushed crude oil sharply higher and US Treasury yields to fresh highs (10-year at 4.79-4.81%, highest in nearly three years), and Taiwan's export-heavy, rate-sensitive tech sector took the direct hit.
- **AI blue chips led the decline**: **TSMC / 台積電 fell −2.05%**, and **Delta Electronics / 台達電 dropped −6.17%** — both among the index's heaviest-weighted constituents, meaning their declines did outsized damage to the headline number.
- **MediaTek / 聯發科 was the lone bright spot among large caps, gaining +0.35%** — the stock continued to draw support from Tuesday's NVIDIA convertible-bond investment news (US$3.5B tied to a 10-year AI-datacenter roadmap; see yesterday's file for the full deal breakdown), even as the rest of the market sold off around it.
- **Foreign investors turned meaningfully bearish in derivatives as well as cash equities**: short positions in TAIEX index futures rose by 6,623 contracts to 85,329 total — a simultaneous bearish bet across spot and futures markets, not just profit-taking after Tuesday's rally.

## Money flow — 三大法人 (three institutional investors)

| Investor | Net flow (Sept 2) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign investors (外資及陸資) | **−NT$91.36B** | The dominant seller by far — a sharp reversal from Tuesday's modest net buying |
| Investment trust (投信) | −NT$3.32B | Comparatively minor, but adds to the same-direction pressure |
| Dealers (自營商) | −NT$20.36B | Includes hedge-position adjustments |
| **Combined** | **−NT$115.03B** | One of the heavier single-day net-sell totals in recent weeks |

Unlike Tuesday's rally — which was concentrated almost entirely in one stock (MediaTek) against only modest net institutional buying — today's selloff was broad-based across the three investor categories and mirrored by rising bearish futures positioning, a more classic "genuine risk-off" signature than a single-stock-driven move.

## Bottom line

A sharp one-day reversal of Tuesday's NVIDIA-driven rally, but the underlying driver isn't Taiwan-specific — it's the same Iran/oil/Treasury-yield thread pressuring US, European, and the rest of Asia's markets this week (see `../260901/usa.md`, `korea.md`, `japan.md`). TSMC and Delta Electronics, as the index's largest weights, did the most damage to the headline number, while MediaTek's continued NVIDIA-deal-driven strength shows the underlying AI-investment story is still intact even as the macro backdrop deteriorates. Worth watching whether foreign investors' NT$91.36B net sell and the jump in bearish futures positioning mark a genuine trend change or a one-day give-back after an outsized single-stock rally.
