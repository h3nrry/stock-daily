# Korea Stock Market Analysis — September 22, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

The **KOSPI closed at 7,017.91, up just 0.15%**, but that headline flatters a session that opened with a **2.2%+ surge (as high as 7,171.44) on overnight US chip strength before individual investors' large-scale selling (개인의 대규모 순매도) erased almost all of it**, with the index briefly dipping to an intraday low of **6,992.70** before clawing back above 7,000 into the close — Korean coverage summarized the day as the index "barely holding the 7,000 line" (7,000선 턱걸이) after "giving back its 2%+ gain" (2%대 급등 후 상승분 반납). The **KOSDAQ fell 0.23% to 834.38**. It was a session of sharp internal rotation rather than a clean move in either direction: **substrate/PCB stocks (기판주) staged a genuine rally** while the large-cap semiconductor names that had led Monday's advance (Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix) gave back much of their own morning spike.

## What drove the session

- **The opening surge was a direct read-through from the US.** Monday's Nasdaq record close, Meta's Muse-AI-agent rally, AMD (+9.95%) and Intel (+12.16%) all featured, and the **Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) jumped 4.29% to 12,433.2** overnight — Kiwoom Securities analyst Han Ji-young (한지영) attributed the early strength to "reduced US-Iran tensions, declining Treasury yields, and Meta's AI success," with falling crude oil also easing macro headwinds.
- **Substrate/PCB names (기판주) were the session's clearest winners, driven by the same US CPU-price-hike story that lifted AMD.** **Korea Circuit (코리아써키트)** led with a **+28.68% surge to ₩67,300**; **TLB +19.82% to ₩45,650**; **Simtec (심텍) +17.98%**; **Interflex (인터플렉스) +16.77%**; **Netis/Neotis +16.65%**; **Daeduck Electronics (대덕전자) +14.92%**. The thesis, per industry commentary: "AI server investment expansion is expected to increase related substrate demand," since premium CPUs and AI accelerators require package substrates connecting chips to mainboards — a close cousin of Taiwan's own ABF/substrate rally the same day (see `taiwan.md`).
- **Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix both faded from steep morning gains.** Samsung Electronics was as high as +2.46% intraday (₩280,750) before closing a more modest **+0.91%**; SK Hynix spiked as much as +3.05% (₩1,925,000) before **reversing to close −1.50%** — a genuine intraday round-trip in the market's largest stock, and the single biggest reason the index gave back its early gain. Samsung Electro-Mechanics (삼성전기) held onto a **+3.99%** gain and Doosan Enerbility (두산에너빌리티) closed **+1.41%**.
- **Individual investors were the dominant sellers.** The KOSPI's late-session fade was explicitly attributed to "large-scale individual net selling" (개인의 대규모 순매도) — a session where retail took profits into the morning's strength, a mirror image of Monday, when individuals had also been the largest net sellers (see `../260921/korea.md`).
- **Wednesday is the last KOSPI session before Chuseok** (KRX closed Sept 24–25) — with Tuesday's round-trip suggesting some of the same pre-holiday de-risking flagged in Taiwan's write-up (see `taiwan.md`) is playing out in Korea too.

Given the index's intraday round-trip, this reads as profit-taking by retail into the morning spike rather than a broad flight from risk.

## Calendar note — last session before Chuseok is Wednesday

Per `../260921/korea.md`: **KRX is closed Sept 24–25 for Chuseok**, reopening Monday Sept 28. Wednesday Sept 23 is therefore the **last KOSPI session before the holiday**, and it falls on the same day as global flash PMI releases — with Thursday's Trump–Xi summit (a stated agenda item: AI-semiconductor export controls) landing entirely inside the market closure, meaning Korean prices will gap on reopening to whatever the summit and the intervening US sessions produce.

## Bottom line

Tuesday's KOSPI (+0.15% to 7,017.91) looked flat on the headline but was a genuinely volatile session underneath: a 2%+ morning surge on US chip-market strength (Nasdaq record, SOX +4.3%) was almost entirely erased by large-scale individual selling, leaving the index barely holding 7,000. The clearest signal of the day was sectoral rotation — substrate/PCB names (Korea Circuit +28.68%, and a broad basket of peers) rallied hard on AI-server-substrate demand even as Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, Monday's leaders, both gave back steep intraday gains. With Wednesday the last session before the Chuseok closure, the combination of poor follow-through in the large-cap chip leaders and heavy retail selling is worth watching into the holiday gap.
