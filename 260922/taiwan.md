# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 22, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

The **TAIEX rose 81.33 points (+0.17%) to close at 47,800.17**, a fifth straight gain and a fresh closing high, but the print badly understates the session's volatility: the index opened at 47,981.04, spiked as much as **882.69 points intraday to 48,601.53** (an all-time intraday high) on record MediaTek and AUO momentum, then gave back the entire advance to close near the day's lows — an **801-point intraday swing**, the kind of round-trip typically associated with pre-holiday profit-taking (賣壓) rather than fresh buying conviction. Trading value was **NT$1,021.569 billion**, the first NT$1-trillion-plus session logged in this project in some time. Breadth was poor for an index at a record: only **608 stocks advanced against 1,156 decliners** — confirmation that Tuesday's gain was a further narrowing of the AI/HPC rally rather than a broadening of it. The three institutional-investor categories were net buyers for a **fourth straight session, combining for +NT$60.821 billion**, with foreign investors alone contributing +NT$44.727 billion (cumulative foreign buying over the streak now exceeds NT$160 billion).

## What drove the session

- **A blow-off morning, then a hard reversal.** MediaTek (聯發科, 2454) hit an intraday record of NT$5,480 (session close NT$5,180, +3.39%) and TSMC (台積電, 2330) briefly touched NT$2,510 before both faded — TSMC actually closed **red, at NT$2,460 (−0.81%, −NT$20)** — a clear signal that the index's late-session slide was led by the two largest weights reversing from their own highs, not just small-cap profit-taking.
- **Substrate/ABF and advanced-packaging names kept extending Monday's theme.** **Unimicron (欣興電子, 3037)** and **South Epitaxy/PCB peer 8046 (中華精測系列)** hit limit-up on AI/HPC substrate demand; **Global Unichip (創意電子, 3443)** hit a fresh record with a limit-up close at **NT$8,375 (+9.98%)**; **Aspeed Technology (信驊科技, 5274)** became **Taiwan's first NT$20,000-plus stock**, closing at NT$20,905 (+5.47%) after an intraday peak of NT$21,800.
- **AUO (友達, 2409) locked limit-up for a second consecutive session**, closing at **NT$36.65 (+9.9%)** on record volume, extending Monday's Intel Micro-LED/CPO-partnership rally (TSMC is also reportedly courting AUO — see `../260921/usa.md`).
- **The reversal itself is the story.** Local coverage (UDN, Newtalk) framed the 801-point round-trip explicitly as 節前賣壓 (pre-holiday selling pressure): with the market closing for four days from Friday (Mid-Autumn Festival Sept 25, Teachers' Day Sept 28), traders who chased Monday's and Tuesday morning's AI-supply-chain surge used the intraday record to lock in gains rather than carry a long AI-heavy book through the closure and Thursday's Trump–Xi summit.
- **Foreign investors kept buying even as the index gave back its gains** — a genuine divergence: the tape (price action) turned cautious intraday, but institutional flow (especially foreign, +NT$44.727 billion, a fourth straight buy day) stayed constructive. That combination — buying on weakness, not chasing the top — is a different signature than Monday's broader net-buying.

## Calendar note — three sessions left before the four-day closure

Per `../260921/taiwan.md`: **TWSE is closed Friday Sept 25 (Mid-Autumn Festival) and Monday Sept 28 (Teachers' Day)**, with **Thursday Sept 24 the last session of the week** — coinciding with the **Trump–Xi summit in Washington**. Tuesday's round-trip session is consistent with traders already positioning for that closure.

## Bottom line

Tuesday was a session of two stories in one print: the TAIEX closed at a fresh record (47,800.17, +0.17%, a fifth straight gain), but only after giving back an 882-point intraday surge — an 801-point round-trip driven by pre-holiday profit-taking in the very AI/HPC names (MediaTek, TSMC, AUO) that drove Monday's rally. Institutional flow stayed constructive throughout (three-institution net buying of NT$60.821 billion, a fourth straight buy day, foreign investors doing the heavy lifting), which argues the pullback was position-trimming rather than a change of view — but deteriorating breadth (608 advancers vs. 1,156 decliners) on a record-high day is a caution flag heading into Thursday's final pre-holiday session and the Trump–Xi summit.
