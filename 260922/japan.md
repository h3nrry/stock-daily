# Japan Stock Market Analysis — September 22, 2026 (Tuesday) — MARKET CLOSED (HOLIDAY, DAY 2 OF 3)

**The Tokyo Stock Exchange stayed closed Tuesday Sept 22 — the "bridge holiday" (国民の休日) between Monday's Respect for the Aged Day (敬老の日) and Wednesday's Autumnal Equinox Day (秋分の日) — and remains closed Wednesday Sept 23. Cash equities resume Thursday, Sept 24.** There is therefore still **no Nikkei 225 or TOPIX cash close for Sept 22** — the "1-day" column for Japan in this folder's `README.md` continues to show the last confirmed cash close (Friday, Sept 18: Nikkei 65,018.95, +1.38%) as n/a for a second straight day. As on Monday, Japan Exchange Group's *derivatives* markets kept trading on their holiday schedule.

## Holiday-session derivatives (Osaka Exchange) — day 2

Nikkei 225 futures (December contract) continued to track the overnight US tape through Tuesday's holiday session, building on Monday's move:

- **Night session (through the early hours of Sept 22): futures rose to 66,380, up 1,280 yen from the Sept 18 futures settlement of 65,100** — driven by the Nasdaq Composite's fresh record close and a **4%+ overnight surge in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX)** on Monday's US session (Meta's Muse AI agent, AMD, Intel — see `../260921/usa.md`).
- **The Tuesday holiday day-session (from 8:45am JST) extended those gains further**, with futures rising **as much as 1,880 yen** intraday (an indicative high near the 66,900–67,000 area) before **paring back roughly 930 yen from that high**, leaving holiday-session trading described by local commentary as settling "in the 66,000-yen range" (祝日取引は6万6000円台).
- ***Data-quality note:*** *the exact holiday-session settlement print for Tuesday was not independently confirmed beyond the qualitative "66,000-yen range" characterization and the +1,880-yen/−930-yen intraday move; treat any single derived number (e.g., ≈66,050) as indicative only, not a confirmed close — consistent with this project's standing caution (see `../260921/japan.md`) that thin holiday-session futures volume can overstate or misstate the eventual cash-market gap.*
- USD/JPY levels for Tuesday's holiday session were not independently confirmed in this research pass; Monday's last confirmed reads were in the **157.06–157.28** range with intervention risk still flagged (see `../260921/japan.md`).

## Bottom line

Japan remains shut for a second straight session, with no new cash-market data to report: the Nikkei's last confirmed close is still Friday's **65,018.95 (+1.38%)**. Holiday-session Nikkei futures continued to grind higher through Tuesday — up 1,280 yen Monday night to 66,380, then as much as 1,880 yen at Tuesday's intraday high before paring back roughly 930 yen — tracking the same US AI-driven optimism (Nasdaq record, SOX +4%+) that lifted Taiwan, Korea and Hong Kong on Tuesday (see `taiwan.md`, `korea.md`, `hongkong.md`). With Wednesday also a holiday, **Thursday's reopening will have to absorb three full sessions of global news at once**, on the same day as the Trump–Xi summit and the BOJ's rate hike taking effect — a genuinely event-dense first trading day back.
