# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 8, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Headline

Taiwan gave back a slice of Monday's record institutional-buying rally. The **TAIEX (加權指數) fell 220.49 points (−0.47%) to close at 47,105.78**, holding the psychologically important 47,000 level despite a volatile **554.51-point intraday swing** between a high of 47,578 and a low of 47,024 — the market opened higher, tracking Monday's own momentum, but faded through the session as renewed US-Iran tension and a pullback in US futures weighed on sentiment. Turnover came in around **NT$876.7 billion**.

## What drove the pullback

- **Breadth and leadership rotated away from Monday's semiconductor mega-caps and into three narrower themes**: (1) **probe-card/AI-testing suppliers** — **WinWay Technology / 穎崴 (6515) hit the daily limit-up (+10%)** to NT$7,700 on continued advanced-chip testing demand; (2) **memory semiconductors** — **Winbond Electronics / 華邦電 and Nanya Technology / 南亞科** both saw heavy turnover on August revenue highs and continued DRAM/NAND pricing momentum; (3) **financials** — **Yuanta Financial Holdings / 元大金 (2884) surged +4.59% to a historic high of NT$45.55** on strong August profitability, leading a broader rally in bank/insurance names.
- **The index's two largest weights diverged**: **TSMC / 台積電 (2330) edged up NT$10 (+0.41%) to NT$2,470**, holding up relatively well, while **MediaTek / 聯發科 (2454) gave back NT$50 (−1.05%) to NT$4,710**, cooling off after Monday's +7.81% surge — a normal pause after such a sharp one-day move rather than a reversal of the underlying AI/data-center-chip thesis.
- **Other large caps softened broadly**: **Hon Hai / 鴻海 (2317) fell NT$4.5 (−1.76%) to NT$251.5**, **Quanta Computer / 廣達 (2382) dropped NT$19.5 (−5.61%) to NT$328** after its own recent run-up, and **Evergreen Marine / 長榮海運 (2603) eased NT$4 (−1.72%) to NT$229**.
- **A genuinely two-way session at the small-cap level**: 永擎 (7711), a recent high-flyer, swung from a same-day limit-up (NT$632) to a limit-down (NT$594) within the session according to intraday wires — an extreme illustration of how speculative Monday's broader rally had become in pockets of the market, even as the headline index gave back less than half a percent.
- **The external backdrop turned more cautious overnight**: Iran and the US traded fresh strikes in the Strait of Hormuz over Monday night (Taiwan time), pushing Brent crude toward $99 intraday and reviving the same geopolitical-risk-off tone that had briefly lifted Taiwan tech names on safe-haven rotation earlier this month — this time it weighed on risk appetite instead, consistent with a broadly softer regional session (see `../260908/japan.md`, `korea.md`).

A sharp reversal from Monday's record NT$113.865 billion institutional net-buy day (see `../260907/taiwan.md`) — but the composition is the real story: **foreign investors kept buying for a third straight session**, while investment trusts and dealers (particularly the hedging book) did the selling. That split is broadly consistent with the day's own price action — a rotation out of Monday's biggest semiconductor winners and into probe-card, memory, and financial names — rather than a wholesale loss of institutional conviction.

## Bottom line

A genuine, but orderly, pullback after Monday's record institutional-buying rally — TAIEX −0.47% to 47,105.78, holding 47,000 despite a volatile 554-point intraday swing. The composition beneath the headline number is constructive rather than alarming: foreign investors extended their buying streak to a third straight day even as investment trusts and dealers took profits, and the session's leadership rotated cleanly into probe-card/testing, memory, and financial names rather than showing a broad loss of risk appetite. The overnight Iran/Strait-of-Hormuz escalation and softer US futures were the clearest external drag. Whether Wednesday brings a return to Monday's semiconductor-led buying or a further rotation into this session's new leadership groups is the clearest thing to watch next.
