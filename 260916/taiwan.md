# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 16, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

The **TAIEX rebounded 337.41 points (+0.74%) to close at 45,848.9**, snapping a four-session losing streak, on trading value that expanded to roughly **NT$643.77 billion**. The index touched an intraday high of **46,077.82 (up over 560 points at the peak)** before investors trimmed gains heading into Wednesday night's FOMC decision — a familiar pattern this project has tracked all week of strength fading somewhat as the Fed decision approaches. **TSMC (台積電, 2330)** closed **flat at NT$2,380** on its ex-dividend date, having quickly "filled" (填息) most of the NT$7-per-share dividend gap that mechanically subtracted an estimated **56.59 points** from the index at the open.

## What drove the rebound

- **A rebound in US semiconductor sentiment carried directly into Taiwan's session.** Reports that **SK Hynix and Intel are in talks over a potential US chip-manufacturing partnership** sent Intel (+5.33%) and SK Hynix's ADR (+3.14%) sharply higher in US premarket trading (see `../260916/usa.md`), and the same AI/semiconductor relief lifted Taiwan's own chip-supply-chain names after Tuesday's broad weakness.
- **TSMC's own session was a genuine bright spot at the mechanical level**: closing flat at NT$2,380 despite the NT$7 ex-dividend adjustment (填息, "filling the ex-dividend gap") on literally its first trading day back — a sign of resilient underlying demand for the stock even as the headline price looks unchanged.
- **MediaTek (聯發科, 2454) rose over 2% to NT$4,530**, and **Largan Precision (大立光, 3008) gained over 2% to NT$6,225** — both among the session's stronger large-cap performers, consistent with the broader AI/thermal-management and memory-sector "ignition" (點火) local media flagged for the session.
- **Foreign investors selectively bought panel, memory, and financial stocks even while remaining net sellers overall** — AU Optronics (友達, AUO, 2409) received the largest foreign net-buy volume of the session (52,473 shares) — a notable divergence from the blanket foreign selling seen across Tuesday's session.
- **Investor caution around the FOMC decision capped the rally's follow-through**: having touched 46,077.82 intraday (+560 points), the index gave back roughly 40% of its peak gain into the close as "investors adopted a cautious stance, watching US Federal Reserve interest rate policy directions" (CNA) rather than chasing the rebound aggressively.

Foreign investors extended their selling streak to a **fifth consecutive session** even as the index rallied — a genuine divergence worth watching, since it suggests Wednesday's rebound was driven more by short-covering and domestic buying (investment trusts turned decisively net buyers, reversing several days of selling) than by a return of foreign conviction. The scale of net institutional selling (≈NT$213.8 billion) against a rising index is a pattern this project has not seen as starkly in recent sessions, and is consistent with the intraday give-back described above — investors bought the early rally, then de-risked into the FOMC decision.

## Bottom line

Wednesday's +0.74% TAIEX rebound snapped a four-session losing streak, but the details complicate a simple "relief rally" read: foreign investors extended their net-selling streak to a fifth day even as the index rose, total institutional flow was solidly negative (≈−NT$213.8 billion) despite the gain, and the index gave back a meaningful chunk of its intraday peak (46,077.82, +560 points) as investors turned cautious heading into Wednesday night's FOMC decision. TSMC's own steady, ex-dividend-day close and the selective foreign buying in AU Optronics and other panel/memory names are the session's more constructive signals — a hint that some investors are already positioning for specific sub-sectors ahead of the Fed's decision rather than making a broad directional bet. **With the FOMC decision landing in the middle of Wednesday night's US session (see `usa.md`), Thursday's Taiwan open will be the first regional market to react to the actual outcome.**
