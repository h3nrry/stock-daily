# Japan Stock Market Analysis — September 16, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

The **Nikkei 225 rose 438.90 points (+0.69%) to close at 63,923.00**, its first rebound in four sessions after a weak morning gave way to a late-session recovery that pushed the index to a high-of-day close. The broader **TOPIX also advanced, up 24.56 points (+0.61%) to 4,061.72** — confirming this was a genuinely broad-based rebound rather than a single-stock-driven headline number, a contrast with Tuesday's SoftBank-flattered flat close (see `../260915/japan.md`).

## What drove the session

- **The session tracked the same overnight relief seen across Asia**: reports of a potential **SK Hynix–Intel US chip-manufacturing partnership** (see `../260916/usa.md` and `../260916/korea.md`) lifted global semiconductor sentiment, and Japan's own chip-adjacent and AI-hardware names participated in the broader market's late-session turnaround.
- **A weak morning gave way to a stronger afternoon** — Kabutan's own headline described the session as "4日ぶり反発、朝安も後半切り返し高値引け" (first rebound in four sessions; weak in the morning but reversed higher in the second half to close near the day's high), a pattern consistent with investors initially cautious ahead of the Fed decision before buying resumed later in the day.
- **Attention across the session remained fixed on the Federal Reserve's policy decision**, due to land in the middle of Wednesday night's US session — the Nikkei's own resilience into the close suggests markets were already positioning for a "hike as expected, no surprise" outcome rather than bracing for a hawkish shock.
- **The TOPIX's own solid 0.61% gain (broadly in line with the Nikkei's 0.69%) is itself a notable contrast with Tuesday**, when the TOPIX fell 0.52% even as the Nikkei closed flat on SoftBank's single-stock rally — Wednesday's session shows genuine across-the-board participation rather than one stock masking broader weakness.

## Money flow — 外国人 (foreign investors)

Same-day foreign-investor flow data was not available at writing time — the Japan Exchange Group (JPX) publishes investor-type statistics weekly rather than daily, a limitation this project has flagged repeatedly. Given the broad-based nature of Wednesday's rebound (TOPIX and Nikkei both up by a similar magnitude, unlike Tuesday's single-stock-driven divergence), genuine buying interest — rather than a single large position — looks like the more plausible driver, but this remains an inference from price action rather than a confirmed flow figure.

## Bottom line

Wednesday's Nikkei rebound (+0.69%) is a more encouraging session than it might first appear: unlike Tuesday's flat close, which masked a 0.52% TOPIX decline behind a single SoftBank rally, Wednesday's gain was matched almost point-for-point by the TOPIX (+0.61%), confirming genuine market-wide participation. The session's own shape — a weak morning followed by a second-half reversal to a high-of-day close — suggests investors grew more comfortable buying into strength as the day wore on, even with the Fed's decision still hours away. With the FOMC outcome landing in the middle of Wednesday night's US session, Thursday's Tokyo open will be the first Japanese session to trade on the Fed's actual decision rather than anticipation of it.
