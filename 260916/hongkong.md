# Hong Kong Stock Market Analysis — September 16, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

The **Hang Seng Index rose 0.19% to close at 24,713.78**, a modest but genuine rebound from Tuesday's broad-based decline (see `../260915/hongkong.md`), as the index "oscillated upward throughout the day" despite headwinds in major tech names. The **Hang Seng TECH Index outperformed, up 0.79% to 4,325.45**, led by strength in semiconductor and optical-communication names even as several megacap internet stocks slipped.

## What drove the session

- **Semiconductor and optical-communication names led the day's gains**, tracking the same SK Hynix–Intel partnership-driven relief lifting chip sentiment across Asia (see `../260916/korea.md`, `../260916/taiwan.md`): **Cambridge Technology (中電科技) surged over 10%**, **Shanghai Fudan (上海復旦) rose over 8%**, and mid-cap tech name **Central Create gained around 6%**.
- **Gold-mining and precious-metals names surged broadly** — a notable reversal from Tuesday's sharp gold-stock declines — as spot gold itself rose on the day (see "Oil/Gold/Bitcoin" in `../260916/README.md`) amid the same pre-FOMC positioning lifting safe-haven demand.
- **Major internet/tech megacaps were the session's weak spot even as the broader index rose**: **Alibaba (阿里巴巴) fell 0.93%, Tencent (騰訊) dropped 1.23%, Xiaomi (小米) fell 1.21%, and Bilibili (嗶哩嗶哩) declined 2.72%** — a genuine divergence between the semiconductor-led gainers and megacap-tech laggards within the same session.
- **Battery names were mixed**, with **Contemporary Amperex Technology (寧德時代, CATL) down nearly 3%**, and the **pharmaceutical sector weakened broadly** — both sectors bucking the day's overall gain.

## Money flow — 南向資金 (southbound Stock Connect)

**Southbound Stock Connect recorded a net inflow of HK$2.099 billion** on Wednesday, marking an **eighth consecutive day of net buying** — Shanghai-linked flows contributed a net HK$2.669 billion while Shenzhen-linked flows saw a modest HK$570 million net outflow. This extended streak of southbound buying, even through Tuesday's broader regional selloff, underscores continued mainland investor demand for Hong Kong-listed names heading into the FOMC decision.

## Bottom line

Wednesday's +0.19% Hang Seng gain, while modest at the headline level, masks a more interesting session underneath: the Hang Seng TECH Index's stronger 0.79% gain reflects a genuine semiconductor-led rally (tracking the same SK Hynix–Intel partnership story lifting Korea and Taiwan) even as megacap internet names (Alibaba, Tencent, Xiaomi, Bilibili) all declined — a clear sector rotation within a single session. Southbound Stock Connect's eighth consecutive day of net buying is a notably persistent signal of mainland demand that has held through both Tuesday's broad selloff and Wednesday's more selective rebound. With the FOMC decision landing in the middle of Wednesday night's US session, Thursday's Hong Kong open will be the first regional session to trade on the Fed's actual outcome.
