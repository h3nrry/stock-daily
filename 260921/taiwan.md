# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 21, 2026 (Monday)

## Headline

The **TAIEX rose 538.09 points (+1.14%) to close at 47,718.84**, its **fourth straight gain (+2,207 points in total since Tuesday's close)** and the highest close in roughly three months per local coverage. The session opened weak on TSMC (台積電, 2330) but **reversed as the AI-supply-chain names took over**: **MediaTek (聯發科, 2454) closed +6.37% at NT$5,010 — its first close above NT$5,000, with a market cap above NT$8 trillion intraday**, **Delta Electronics (台達電, 2308) surged +8.07% to NT$1,875**, and **TSMC recovered to close +0.81% at NT$2,480** (about 159 index points of contribution). Trading value was **NT$820.215 billion**; the intraday high was 47,750.98, i.e. the index closed almost on its high. The OTC index (櫃買) was essentially flat (−0.03% to 412.57), so this was a large-cap, electronics-led rally rather than a broad-market one. Institutional money was decisively net-buying: **+NT$47.038 billion combined** across the three institutional-investor categories.

## What drove the session

- **AI/ASIC and advanced-packaging leadership.** Local coverage tied the rally to US AI-stock strength (Nasdaq, S&P 500 and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index all higher on Friday — see `usa.md` for the current US session) and to an ASIC-demand story that lit up **MediaTek**; related IC-design names (點序, 精拓科, 倚強科) were reported at daily limit-up. UDN named the leading themes as **advanced packaging, CCL (copper-clad laminate), ASIC, thermal management and ABF substrates**.
- **Three heavyweights carried the index.** TSMC, MediaTek and Delta each contributed roughly 100+ index points (TSMC ≈159), which is why a +1.14% index gain came with only mixed breadth in the smaller OTC market.
- **AUO (友達, 2409) locked limit-up at NT$33.35 (+9.88%) on record volume (over 510,000 lots / 51 million shares)** after reports that **Intel is interested in partnering with AUO on Micro-LED substrate advanced packaging, co-packaged optics (CPO) and high-density compute integration**; peer **Innolux (群創, 3481)** rose more than 4%. Note this is a *reported/rumoured* partnership — the same story lifted Intel in the US premarket (see `usa.md`).
- **Packaging/test names broadened the move**: ChipMOS (南茂, 8150) and Walton Advanced Engineering (華東, 8110) hit limit-up, KYEC (京元電子, 2449) and ASE Technology (日月光投控, 3711) rose roughly 3% or more.
- **Memory chips lagged**: Winbond (華邦電, 2344) fell more than 3%, Powerchip (力積電, 6770) and Nanya Technology (南亞科, 2408) more than 1%, and Macronix (旺宏, 2337) about 0.8%, after commentary that DDR4/DDR5 supply is normalising and Chinese capacity is rising — a divergence from Korea's memory-heavy KOSPI the same day (see `korea.md`).
- **Construction stocks gave back gains** (京城 down about 6%, several others down 3%+) — profit-taking after a run tied to central-bank credit-policy changes.
- **Foreign futures positioning:** foreign investors' net short in TAIEX futures narrowed by 2,029 contracts to **74,081 contracts net short** — still a large hedge, meaning the rally has been chased with hedges on rather than with an all-in long book.

This is roughly **2.2x Thursday's +NT$21.477 billion** (see `../260917/taiwan.md`) The composition matters: **about half of Monday's buying came from dealers, and roughly half of *that* is hedging-account buying** (which is often options/warrant-related market-making flow rather than directional conviction), so the "clean" directional buyers — foreigners and trusts — contributed about NT$24.3 billion. That is still a genuine positive: foreign investors were net buyers on Thursday (+NT$12.185 billion) and again Monday; Friday's split was not gathered in this session (see follow-up note in `README.md`). The OTC market, by contrast, saw **three-institution net *selling* of NT$9.982 billion** — consistent with capital concentrating in the large-cap electronics complex.

## Calendar note — shortened week

**TWSE is closed Friday Sept 25 (Mid-Autumn Festival) and Monday Sept 28 (Teachers' Day)**, with the last session of the week on **Thursday Sept 24** and the market reopening **Tuesday Sept 29** (a four-day closure including the weekend). Only **three trading sessions remain (Sept 22–24)**, and the Thursday session coincides with the **Trump–Xi summit in Washington** — historically a reason for traders to shorten exposure into a long closure.

## Bottom line

Monday was a strong, concentrated, institution-backed AI-supply-chain rally: the TAIEX closed at 47,718.84 (+1.14%), a fourth straight gain and a three-month high, with **MediaTek and Delta** doing the heavy lifting, TSMC recovering from a soft open, and the three institutional categories net-buying NT$47.038 billion. Two cautions worth keeping in view: roughly half of the day's buying was hedging-account dealer flow, and the rally is narrow (memory chips, construction and OTC lagged). With the market shut for four days from Sept 25, the next three sessions are likely to be event-driven (Micron results, PMI data, the Trump–Xi summit).
