# Korea Stock Market Analysis — September 21, 2026 (Monday)

## Headline

The **KOSPI closed at 7,007.72, up 113.49 points (+1.65%)**, **reclaiming the 7,000 level for the first time in seven trading days**, with an intraday high of 7,027.58. The **KOSDAQ rose 9.15 points (+1.11%) to 836.27**. Trading value was about **₩19.69 trillion** on roughly 232.8 million shares. The rally was **extremely narrow**: on the KOSPI only about **310 stocks advanced against 552 decliners**, and on the KOSDAQ **630 advanced against 1,021 decliners** — a classic mega-cap-led index gain in which the semiconductor heavyweights, and above all **Samsung Electronics (삼성전자, 005930)**, did all the work.

## What drove the session

- **Samsung Electronics took the lead over SK Hynix.** Samsung Electronics closed **+4.98% at ₩274,000** and **Samsung Electronics Preferred (삼성전자우) +6.07% at ₩208,000**; Samsung Electronics turnover was about ₩5.85 trillion. Korean coverage (WikiTree) framed Samsung as having displaced **SK Hynix (SK하이닉스, 000660)** as the market's lead stock; SK Hynix itself was only modestly higher (+0.75% to about ₩1,871,000 in the afternoon read). **SK Square (SK스퀘어) +4.45% to ₩1,127,000** and **Samsung Electro-Mechanics (삼성전기) +2.95% to ₩1,429,000** joined the advance.
- **Outside the chip complex it was weak.** **LG Energy Solution (LG에너지솔루션) fell 3.30% to ₩352,000** and **Hyundai Motor (현대차) fell 1.64% to ₩359,000** — the source of the negative breadth.
- **Global backdrop was supportive**: AI-related stocks rallied in the US on Friday and again in Monday's US session (see `usa.md`), oil kept falling (Brent's fourth straight decline), and Bessent-led US–China talks ahead of Thursday's Trump–Xi summit were described as "very successful" — all favourable to a chip-exporter market. The **won/dollar closed at about ₩1,381.0 (₩2.3 stronger)** per Seoul Shinmun; one weekly-preview outlet quoted a different level near ₩1,330, so treat the FX figure as unconfirmed.
- **Wednesday is the last session before Chuseok.** Korean strategists quoted historical data showing the five sessions before the holiday average a −0.42% return versus +0.68% after it — Monday's move ran against that pattern.

## Money flow — 외국인 / 기관 / 개인 (foreign / institutional / individual)

Final KRX figures (Seoul Shinmun, confirmed against WikiTree):

| Investor group | Net flow (KOSPI, Sept 21) |
|---|---|
| Individuals (개인) | **−₩2.9790 trillion** (net sell) |
| Foreigners (외국인) | **−₩171.9 billion** (small net sell) |
| Institutions (기관) | **+₩1.4924 trillion** (net buy) |

**Individuals were the big sellers and institutions the buyers — the reverse of last week's pattern**, in which foreign selling (a seven-session streak through Thursday, see `../260917/korea.md`) had been the dominant story. Note the **intraday-versus-final divergence**: at about 2pm, Financial News (fnnews) reported foreigners as net *buyers* (≈₩559 billion) with "other corporations" buying ≈₩1.404 trillion and institutions ≈₩1.269 trillion; by the close foreigners had flipped to a small net sell. That kind of late reversal is why this project prefers the explicitly end-of-day-labelled figures. Foreign flows on Friday (Sept 18) were not gathered in this session, so the continuation of the selling streak cannot be confirmed here.

## Calendar note — Chuseok closure

**KRX is closed Sept 24–25 for Chuseok** and reopens **Monday Sept 28**; only **three sessions (Sept 21–23) fall in this week**. That puts the **Trump–Xi summit (Sept 24, Washington)** — where AI-semiconductor export controls are a stated agenda item — entirely inside the market holiday, so Korean prices will gap on Sept 28 to whatever the summit and the intervening US sessions produce.

## Bottom line

Monday's KOSPI gain (+1.65% to 7,007.72) was a **Samsung Electronics-led, institution-backed, narrow rally** that restored the psychologically important 7,000 level despite net foreign selling and poor breadth. It is a constructive signal for the chip-heavy index, but the combination of (1) negative breadth, (2) individuals net-selling ₩2.98 trillion, and (3) a Micron print and a market holiday just ahead means conviction is unproven. Versus this project's reference points, the index is **+4.84% over five sessions (vs. 6,684.37 on Sept 14) and +4.64% over roughly 20 sessions (vs. 6,696.96 on Aug 24)**.
