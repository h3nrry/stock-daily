# Japan Stock Market Analysis — September 10, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

Japan's Nikkei 225 staged a dramatic intraday reversal, tumbling nearly 1,000 points before rallying hard into the close to finish higher for a second straight session. The index **closed at 65,270.95, up 128.17 points (+0.19%)**, after touching an **intraday low of 64,186.68 at 10:36am** — a genuine V-shaped session rather than a quiet one, and still down 182.83 points as late as 2:33pm before surging through the final stretch. The close reconciles exactly against Wednesday's confirmed 65,142.78 (see `../260909/japan.md`).

## What drove the session

- **The morning plunge tracked the same deteriorating Middle East backdrop hitting markets globally** (中東情勢悪化) — oil's push toward $105/barrel and fresh US-Iran tension around the Strait of Hormuz (see `../260910/usa.md`, `hongkong.md`) — alongside **a Bank of Japan official's comments reaffirming a continued rate-hike path**, which nudged the yen slightly weaker to around **153.4 per dollar**.
- **The index recovered essentially all of its loss and then some in the final stretch of trading**: still down 182.83 points at 2:33pm (64,959.95), the Nikkei surged through the last half-hour to close at the day's high, up 128.17 points — a swing of well over 1,000 points from the session's intraday low to its close.
- **Local commentary linked the sharp rebound partly to a technical tailwind**: the arbitrage buying balance (裁定買い残) fell to its **lowest level in 10 months**, meaning less overhang of stock available for future arbitrage-related selling — a supportive backdrop for a bounce once the morning's panic-selling faded.
- **Company-specific news added support at the margin**: at least one earnings-related name drew heavy buying to a limit-up (ストップ高) after raising its profit forecast roughly 3.3x and lifting its dividend from ¥10 to ¥160 per share.

## Money flow — 外国人 (foreign investors)

Same-day foreign-investor flow data via JPX's weekly investor-type statistics was not available at writing time, as usual for this file (JPX publishes weekly, not daily). Thursday's price action — a violent intraday swing from panic-selling to a technical, low-overhang-driven rebound — is more consistent with short-covering and dip-buying dominating the session than with any confirmed shift in foreign-investor positioning.

## Bottom line

A genuinely wild session that ended up looking calm on the surface — Nikkei +0.19% to 65,270.95 masks an intraday round-trip of nearly 1,100 points, from a 10:36am low of 64,186.68 driven by deteriorating Middle East headlines to a day's-high close. A low arbitrage-buying overhang (a 10-month low) appears to have provided real technical support for the final-hour rebound, even as a Bank of Japan official's continued-rate-hike comments kept the yen on its own separate, slightly weaker path. With the same global oil/yield/Iran backdrop driving nearly every market covered in this folder Thursday, Japan's V-shaped session is arguably the clearest single illustration of just how two-sided and headline-sensitive trading has become this week.
