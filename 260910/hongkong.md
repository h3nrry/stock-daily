# Hong Kong Stock Market Analysis — September 10, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index fell for a fourth consecutive session, breaking below the psychologically important 25,000 level to hit a six-week low. The index **closed at 24,954.47, down approximately 320 points (−1.27%)**, while the tech-heavy **Hang Seng Tech Index fell more sharply, down 2.04% to 4,330.49**. The close reconciles closely against Wednesday's confirmed 25,274.96 (see `../260909/hongkong.md`).

## What drove the decline

- **The same US-Iran Strait of Hormuz escalation driving nearly every market this week intensified further**: commercial vessel traffic through the strait fell to its lowest level since May, and **Brent crude surged as much as 4% intraday to $101.84/barrel** (later prints put Brent above $105 — see `../260910/usa.md`), with **WTI climbing to $96.61**, a 3.5-month high.
- **Technology and AI-application names led the decline**: **Zhipu / 智谱 fell roughly 8–10.5%** (sources diverge slightly) and **MiniMax-W fell about 7%**, while large-cap internet names were broadly weaker — **Alibaba and Meituan both down more than 3%, Baidu down more than 2%, Tencent down nearly 2%** — a much more uniformly negative tech tape than Wednesday's genuinely mixed session.
- **Beyond technology, software applications, power equipment, semiconductors, and heavy machinery sectors all retreated** — a broad-based rather than narrow decline.
- A local fund manager (Li Mingde) flagged **continuously shrinking trading volume** as a sign the market "lacks direction," with foreign investors withdrawing funds; the **25,200 level was cited as key support**, whose failure could open a path toward 24,600.
- Same-day southbound (Stock Connect) net-flow figures could not be confirmed from sources checked at writing time — an honest data gap, consistent with recent sessions.

## Money flow — Stock Connect (港股通 / 南向資金)

Same-day southbound net-buy/sell data was not confirmed from sources checked. Thursday's price action — a broad, largely uniform decline led by AI-application names and spreading into large-cap internet, software, and industrial sectors — reads as a genuine risk-off session rather than one confined to a single story, consistent with the fund-manager commentary about foreign investors withdrawing funds generally.

## Bottom line

A fourth straight losing session that pushed the Hang Seng below the psychologically important 25,000 level to a six-week low — down 1.27% to 24,954.47 — as the US-Iran Strait of Hormuz standoff intensified further and Brent crude pushed toward its highest levels since May. Unlike Wednesday's genuinely mixed technology tape, Thursday's decline was far more uniform: AI-application names (Zhipu, MiniMax-W) led a broad selloff that also caught large-cap internet bellwethers (Alibaba, Meituan, Baidu, Tencent) and spread into software, semiconductors, power equipment, and heavy machinery. With shrinking volume flagged locally as a sign of directionless, foreign-investor-light trading, the 25,200 support level — and whether it holds — is the clearest thing to watch into Friday.
