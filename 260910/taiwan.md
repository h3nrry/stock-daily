# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 10, 2026 (Thursday)

## Headline

Taiwan's TAIEX fell for the first time in two sessions, whipsawed by a sharp intraday plunge tied to a lukewarm investor reaction to Apple's new iPhone lineup and a broader risk-off backdrop of surging oil prices and rising Treasury yields. The index **closed at 46,940.49, down 242.87 points (−0.51%)**, after tumbling more than **600 points intraday to a low of 46,573.47** — briefly testing the 10-day moving average — before recovering roughly half that loss into the close. Turnover came in at **NT$715.95 billion**. The close reconciles cleanly against Wednesday's confirmed 47,183.36 (see `../260909/taiwan.md`).

## What drove the decline

- **Apple's autumn hardware event** (iPhone Duo foldable, iPhone 18 Pro, held Wednesday US time — see `../260910/usa.md`) triggered a "conservative sales outlook" reaction among Taiwanese investors, a sharp reversal from Wednesday's optimism that had lifted Apple-supply-chain optics names. The reaction pressured the broader electronics supply chain Thursday: **TSMC / 台積電 (2330) fell NT$25 to NT$2,440**, with **Hon Hai / 鴻海 (2317)**, **Delta Electronics / 台達電 (2308, −6.69%)**, and **Yageo / 國巨** also weak, dragging the electronics sub-index down more than 1%.
- **Traditional/"傳產" sectors were broadly weak too**: textiles, machinery, construction, and shipping each fell more than 1% — a broad-based decline rather than one confined to tech.
- **MediaTek / 聯發科 (2454) bucked the trend, rising 2.05%**, and **paper stocks moved higher** against the broader tide — isolated pockets of relative strength in an otherwise soft tape.
- **The same-day macro backdrop stayed genuinely risk-off globally**: Brent crude pushed toward $105/barrel (see `../260910/usa.md`) and US Treasury yields extended their climb, while the **European Central Bank raised its policy rate 25 basis points** to defend against energy-driven inflation (see `../260910/uk_europe.md`) — reinforcing the "higher for longer" narrative pressuring risk assets globally, the same backdrop that hit nearly every market in this folder Thursday.
- The roughly 50% recovery from the intraday low into the close suggests index-level support emerged once the market retested the 10-day moving average, even as the underlying sector story (Apple supply chain, traditional sectors) stayed genuinely negative through the close.

## Bottom line

A genuinely negative session beneath a still-orderly-looking headline number — TAIEX −0.51% to 46,940.49 despite a 600-plus-point intraday plunge that tested the 10-day moving average before recovering about half its loss. Apple's underwhelming-to-investors iPhone Duo/iPhone 18 Pro launch reversed Wednesday's optics-driven optimism and instead pressured the broader electronics supply chain (TSMC, Delta, Hon Hai), compounding an already risk-off macro backdrop of oil pushing toward $105, rising Treasury yields, and a fresh ECB rate hike. Foreign investors' abrupt reversal — from more than NT$209 billion of buying Wednesday to over NT$38 billion of selling Thursday, ending a four-day streak — is the clearest institutional confirmation of the day's changed tone. Whether Thursday's intraday low holds as support, and whether the electronics supply chain stabilizes once Apple's actual sales data starts to come in, are the clearest things to watch into Friday.
