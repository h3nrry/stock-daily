# Taiwan Stock Market Analysis — September 9, 2026 (Wednesday)

## Headline

Taiwan's TAIEX (加權指數) eked out a modest gain, closing at **47,183.36, up 77.58 points (+0.16%)**, in a session with roughly a **490-point intraday swing** as institutional buying stabilized the market even as TSMC reversed from gains to a loss. Turnover came in at **NT$773.5 billion**. The close reconciles cleanly against Tuesday's confirmed 47,105.78 (see `../260908/taiwan.md`).

## What drove the gain

- **A "dual boards" (雙板) rotation story**: panel makers **AU Optronics / 友達 (2409) rose 3.04% to NT$30.5** and **Innolux / 群創 (3481) gained 2.66% to NT$50.1**, while substrate/PCB names **Nan Ya PCB / 南亞電路板 (8046) surged 8.29%** and **Unimicron / 欣興電子 (3037) added 4.09%** — this rotation, alongside continued institutional buying, provided the bulk of the index's support.
- **TSMC / 台積電 (2330) reversed from an earlier gain to close down NT$5 (−0.20%) at NT$2,465** in afternoon trading — a swing local wires said dragged the broader index down as much as 45 points intraday before the panel/substrate/optics rotation pulled it back into the green. TSMC's own upcoming ex-dividend date (Sept 16, NT$7/share) was also on investors' radar.
- **Apple-supply-chain optics names ("雙光") rallied ahead of Apple's autumn product event** (held the same day US time, where Apple unveiled its first foldable iPhone Duo and new iPhone 18 Pro models — see `../260909/usa.md`): **Largan Precision / 大立光 (3008) and Genius Electronic Optical / 玉晶光 (3406)** both showed notable strength on optimism around Apple's new hardware cycle.
- **Financials broadly lagged**: all 11 major financial sub-sector indices closed lower, including **Cathay Financial Holdings / 國泰金** and **Fubon Financial Holdings / 富邦金** — a rotation away from financials contrasting with their leadership in Tuesday's session (see `../260908/taiwan.md`).
- **MediaTek / 聯發科 (2454)** and other large caps helped offset TSMC's afternoon weakness, holding the broader index in positive territory despite the volatile 490-point intraday range.
- **The overnight backdrop remained a genuine geopolitical risk-off one**: the US and Iran escalated further in the Strait of Hormuz overnight (Taiwan time) — the US military destroyed five Iranian oil tankers in retaliation for an Iranian ballistic-missile strike on US naval vessels — pushing Brent crude above $100/barrel for the first time since July and driving a broadly negative US session (see `../260909/usa.md`), even as Taiwan itself held up thanks to its own domestic institutional-buying and sector-rotation story.

The scale of this buying, concentrated in panel/substrate names per the session's sector story, is consistent with a market absorbing TSMC's afternoon reversal without losing its overall footing.

## Bottom line

A genuinely constructive session beneath a modest headline number — TAIEX +0.16% to 47,183.36 despite a 490-point intraday swing and TSMC's afternoon reversal to a loss. The real story is the scale of institutional buying (NT$220.81 billion net, the largest since Monday's record) and a clean sector rotation into panel, substrate/PCB, and Apple-supply-chain optics names that more than offset TSMC's weakness and a broadly lower financial sector. That this happened against a genuinely risk-off overnight backdrop — a fresh US-Iran escalation and oil above $100/barrel for the first time since July — makes Taiwan's resilience notable relative to Wednesday's sharply negative US and European sessions (see `../260909/usa.md`, `uk_europe.md`). Whether TSMC's reversal was a one-off or the start of renewed weight-stock pressure is the clearest thing to watch into Thursday.
